Let $\gamma:[0,2\pi]\rightarrow \C$ be the closed smooth path given by
$\gamma(t)=\sum_{n=-N}^Na(n)e^{int}$, where $a(-N)$, $a(-N+1)$,
$\ldots,a(N)$ are complex numbers such that $a(-N)$ or $a(N)$ is nonzero.

For each $z\in \C\setminus \{\gamma(t)\pipe 0\leq t\leq 2\pi\}$, let
$\mathrm{Ind}(z;\gamma)$ denote the index (\ie\ winding number) of
$\gamma$ about $z$. Show that $-N\leq \mathrm{Ind}(z;\gamma)\leq N$.
