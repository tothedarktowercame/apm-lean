Let $f(x)=x^3-7$ in each part below.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $K$ be the splitting field of $f(x)$ over the field of
rational numbers $\Q$. Describe the Galois group $\text{Gal}(K/\Q)$

intermediate fields are not Galois over $\Q$?

numbers $\R$. Describe the Galois group $\text{Gal}(L/\R)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*

with $13$ elements $\mathbf{F}_{13}$. Describe the Galois group
$\text{Gal}(M/\mathbf{F}_{13})$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
