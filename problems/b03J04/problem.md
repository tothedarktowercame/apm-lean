## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $p$ be a prime number and let $k$ be a field. Let $\beta\in k$ be
a nonzero element of $k$ and let $K$ be a splitting field over $k$ for
the polynomial $f(x)=x^p-\beta$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $K$ contains a subfield $K_1$ that is a splitting 
field over $k$ for the polynomial $x^p-1$ and that if $\alpha$ is a
root of $f(x)$ in $K$, then $K=K_1(\alpha)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that either $f(x)$ is irreducible in $k[x]$ or $f(x)$ has
a root in $k$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
