## Problem Statement

Let $V$ be a closed nonzero subspace of a Hilbert space $H$. Prove
that the mapping $F$ that associates with each point in $H$ its unique
closest point in $V$ is (a) linear; (b) of norm $1$; (c) self-adjoint;
