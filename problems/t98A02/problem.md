## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
In what follows, all spaces are assumed connected. You may also assume
that if $N$ is a closed, connected, oriented $4$-manifold, then
$H_4(N,\Z)\cong \Z$.

Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ be connected oriented $4$-manifolds and $Y$ their
connect sum, \ie\ letting $D^4$ denote the $4$-disk and
$\varphi_i:D^4\rightarrow X_i$ for $i=1,2$ embeddings, form $Y$ as the
identification space by identifying 
$\varphi_1(\partial D^4)$ in $X_1
\setminus \varphi_1(D^4)$ with 
$\varphi_2(\partial D^4)$ in $X_2
\setminus \varphi_2(D^4)$.  

You may assume that $\varphi_i(D^4)$ is a $4$-simplex in the
triangulation of $X_i$. Compute all homology groups of $Y$ in terms of
those for $X_1$ and $X_2$.
```
