## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let $S$ be a multiplicative set in
$A$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $M$ is a module over $A$, define what $S^{-1}M$ means.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $M,N$ and $Q$ are modules over $A$ and the sequence
$0\rightarrow M\rightarrow N\rightarrow Q\rightarrow 0$ is exact, show
that the sequence $0\rightarrow S^{-1} M \rightarrow S^{-1}N
\rightarrow S^{-1}Q\rightarrow 0$ is exact.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give an example to show that the converse of (b) does
not hold.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
