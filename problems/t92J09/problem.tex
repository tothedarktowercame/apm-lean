Let $\Stw$ be the sphere of radius $1$ centered at the origin in
$\Rth$.  Let $\omega$ be the $2$-form $x dy\wedge dz$ where $x,y$ and
$z$ are the usual coordinates on $\Rth$. If $\Rth$ is given the usual
right-handed orientation and $\Stw$ is given the induced orientation
as the boundary of the $3$-ball and if $i:\Stw\rightarrow \Rth$ is the
inclusion map, compute $\int_{\Stw}i^*(\omega)$.
