## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $A$ and $B$ be two distinct points in the circle $\Son$. Let $X$
be the space obtained from $\Son$ and the $2$-sphere $\Stw$ by
identifying $A$ with the north pole in $\Stw$ and $B$ with the south
pole in $\Stw$. Assume that $Y$ is a connected $2$-fold covering of
$X$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Draw pictures of the possible spaces $Y$ could be.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute the homology groups in all dimensions for the spaces in
part (a).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute the fundamental groups for the spaces in part (a).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
