## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
For each positive integer $i$, let $M_i$ be a smooth manifold and let
$f_i:M_x\rightarrow \R^m$ be a smooth map. Show that there is a point
$x\in \Rm$ such that $\forall i: f_i^{-1}(x)$ is a smooth submanifold
of $M_i$.
```
