## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Consider the piecewise linear function $f$ defined on the interval
$[0,1]$ by $f(0)=0$, $f(1/n)=0$ for $n=1,2,3,\ldots$, and
$f(1/2n+1/(2n+2))=1/n$. Prove that $f$ satisfies a relation
$|f(x)-f(y)|\leq A|x-y|^{1/2}$ for all $x,y\in [0,1]$ and some
constant $A$ (that is, $f$ is H\"older continuous with exponent $1/2$
on $[0,1]$) but that $f$ is not absolutely continuous on $[0,1]$.
```
