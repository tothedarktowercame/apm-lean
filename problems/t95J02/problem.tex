Recall that a \emph{retraction} of a space $X$ onto a subspace $A$ is
a map $r:X\rightarrow A$ such that $r|A=\id_A$. Let $X$ be the Klein
bottle and let $A$ and $B$ be the two circles in the standard drawing
of $X$ as a square with identifications. Show:
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item There is a retraction of $X$ onto $A$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item There is no retraction of $X$ onto $B$. Hint: consider the map
$r_{*}:\pi_1(X)\rightarrow \pi_1(B)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
