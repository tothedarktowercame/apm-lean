Let $1<p<\infty$ and let $1/p+1/q=1$. If $f\in L^p(\R)$ and $g\in
L^q(\R)$, define the \emph{convolution of $f$ and $g$} $f*g:\R\rightarrow \R$
by $f*g(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x-y)g(y)dy$. Prove that 
$\forall f\in L^p(\R):\forall g\in L^q(\R): f*g$ is continuous
and $\lim_{|x|\rightarrow \infty}f*g(x)=0$.
