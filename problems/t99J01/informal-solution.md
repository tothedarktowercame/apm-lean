# APM t99J01: Finite covers of a three-circle wedge

Let $X=S^1\vee S^1\vee S^1$. It is a connected graph with
$\pi_1(X)\cong F_3$. For every $n\geq1$ there is a connected $n$-sheeted
cover. For example, take $n$ vertices arranged cyclically; lift the first loop
as the cycle through all vertices and lift each of the other two loops as a
loop at every vertex. Every vertex then has one incoming and outgoing edge of
each label, which is exactly the covering condition.

Euler characteristic multiplies under finite covers. Since $\chi(X)=1-3=-2$,
an $n$-sheeted connected cover $\widetilde X$ has

$$
\chi(\widetilde X)=-2n.
$$

A connected finite graph with fundamental group of rank $r$ has
$\chi=1-r$. Thus $r=2n+1$, and Nielsen--Schreier gives the same conclusion:

$$
\pi_1(\widetilde X)\cong F_{2n+1}.
$$

There can be many nonisomorphic $n$-sheeted covers, corresponding to conjugacy
classes of index-$n$ subgroups of $F_3$ (or transitive permutation actions on
$n$ letters). Nevertheless, every connected one has the same abstract
fundamental group $F_{2n+1}$; different free groups of that rank do not occur.
If disconnected covers are allowed, each component of degree $d$ has group
$F_{2d+1}$, so a partition of the total degree can produce a different list of
component groups.
