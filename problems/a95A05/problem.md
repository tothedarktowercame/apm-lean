## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
If a function $f$ is analytic in the open disk $\Delta=\{z \pipe |z|<1\}$
and continuous in the closure of $\Delta$ and if $f(z)=0$ for every
$z$ in $\partial \Delta$ with $\Im(z)\geq 0$ then $f(z)=0$ for every
$z$ in $\Delta$. Prove this.
```
