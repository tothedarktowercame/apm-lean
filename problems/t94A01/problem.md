## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
For $i=1,2,3,\ldots $, let $X_i=[0,1]$ and let
$X=\prod_{i=1}^{\infty}X_i$. For $f,g\in X$ define the metric
$\rho(f,g)=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}(1/2)^i|f_i-g_i|$. Show that the metric
topology and the product topology on $X$ are the same.
```
