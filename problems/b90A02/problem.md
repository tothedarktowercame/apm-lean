## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a ring with unity. Define a right $R$-module $F_R$ to be
flat if $F\otimes A\rightarrow F\otimes B\rightarrow F\otimes C$ is
exact whenever $A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C$ is an exact sequence of
left $R$-modules.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that free modules are flat.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that projective modules are flat.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $\Q$ is flat as a $\Z$-module.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Is $\Q$ projective as a $\Z$-module. Justify your answer.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
