## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $G$ be a nonabelian group of order $p^3$ where $p$ is a
prime. Prove that the center $Z(G)$ of $G$ is of order $p$ and that
$Z(G)=[G,G]$ (\ie\ $Z(G)$ equals the commutator subgroup of $G$,
namely the group generated by $\{xyx^{-1}y^{-1} \pipe x,y\in G\}$).
```
