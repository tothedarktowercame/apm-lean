## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\pi:X\rightarrow Y$ be a continuous surjection. Suppose that
$\pi^{-1}$ is compact for all $y\in Y$ and that $\pi$ is closed.
Show that if $X$ is Hausdorff, then so is $Y$.
```
