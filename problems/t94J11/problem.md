## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\sim $ be an equivalence relation on a topological space $X$. The
set of equivalence classes of $\sim $ form the \emph{quotient space}
$X/\mathord{\sim }$ endowed with the topology $U\subset
X/\mathord{\sim }$ is open if and only if $\pi^{-1}(U)$ is open, where
$\pi:X\rightarrow X/\mathord{\sim }$ is the projection taking each
point in $X$ to its equivalence class in $X/\mathord{\sim }$. The
relation $\sim $ is \emph{open} if and only if $\pi$ is an open map.
Let $\sim $ be an open equivalence relation. 

Show that $R=\{(x,y)\pipe x\sim y\}$ is a closed subset of the space
$X\times X$ if and only if the quotient space $X/\mathord{\sim }$ is
Hausdorff.
```
