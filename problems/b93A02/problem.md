## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unity. We say ideals $I,J\subset R$
are \emph{relatively prime} if $I+J=R$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $I,J\subset R$ are relatively prime, show that $R/(I\cap
J)\cong R/I\oplus R/J$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For the rest of the problem, assume that $R$ is Artinian.

Suppose that $R$ is an integral domain. Show that $R$ is a
field. Hint: suppose $0\neq x\in R$ and consider the ideals
$(x)\supset(x^2)\supset\ldots $.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that every prime ideal of $R$ is maximal.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose $P_1,\ldots ,P_n$ are distinct prime ideals of $R$. Show
that $P_1\cap \cdots \cap P_{n-1}$ and $P_n$ are relatively prime.
Hint: since $P_n$ is maximal it suffices to find an element of
$P_1\cap \cdots P_{n-1}$ not in $P_n$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $R$ has only finitely many primes.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
