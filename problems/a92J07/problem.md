## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $u,v:\Rtw\rightarrow \R$ be continuously differentiable functions
satisfying the differential equations $u_x=v_y, u_y=-v_x$. Suppose
that for all $x,y\in \R$, $v(x,y)=0$ implies that $u(x,y)>0$. Prove
that $u$ and $v$ are constant.
```
