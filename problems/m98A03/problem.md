## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X,Y$ be Banach spaces and let $U\subset X$ be an open subset. Let
$F:U\rightarrow Y$ be a (not necessarily linear) operator.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define what it means for $F$ to be Fr\'echet differentiable at
$x\in U$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $X=C([0,1])$ be the space of bounded continuous real-valued
functions on $[0,1]$. For $u\in X$ let
$F(u)(x)=\int_0^1K(x,y)f(u(y))dy$, where $K:[0,1]\times
[0,1]\rightarrow \R$ is continuous and $f$ is a $C^1$ mapping of $\R$
into $\R$. Find the Fr\'echet derivative of $F$ at a point $u_0\in X$
and prove that it satisfies the definition (of...).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For the operator $F$ above, is the mapping $u\mapsto DF(u)$
continuous? Prove it is or offer a counterexample.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
