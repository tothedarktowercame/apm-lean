## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a Hilbert space and let $A$ be a linear map of $X$ into
$X$.  (Do not assume that $A$ is continuous.) Let $B$ be a map of $X$
into $X$ such that $\langle Ax,y \rangle=\langle x, By \rangle$ for
all $x$ and $y$ in $X$. (Do not assume that $B$ is linear or
continuous.) Prove that (a) $B$ is linear; (b) $B$ is continuous; and,
(c) $A$ is continuous.
```
