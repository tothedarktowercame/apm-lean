## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Consider the map $f:\RP^3\rightarrow \RP^3$ defined by
$[x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3]\mapsto [x_0,2x_1,3x_2,4x_3]$. Find the fixed points
of $f$. For each fixed point, compute the local Lefschetz
number. Compute the global Lefschetz number $L(f)$.
```
