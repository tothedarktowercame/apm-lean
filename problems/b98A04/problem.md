## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a ring which is left artinian, meaning that it has the
descending chain condition on left ideals. Let $M$ be a left $R$
module which is faithful. \Ie\ if $r\in R$ and $rM=(0)$ then $r=0$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $m_1,m_2\ldots ,m_k\in M$. Show that $\{r\in R\pipe
r m_i=0, 1\leq i\leq k\}$ is a left ideal of $R$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show there are finitely many elements $m_1,m_2,\ldots ,m_s\in M$
such that $\{r\in R\pipe rm_i=0,1\leq i\leq s\}=0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $R$ is module-isomorphic to a submodule of $M\oplus
M\oplus \cdots \oplus M$ for some finite number of copies of $M$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose $0=N_0\subset N_1\subset \cdots \subset N_t=M$ is a
composition series for $M$. Suppose also that $V$ is a simple left
$R$-module. Show that $V$ is module isomorphic to $N_i/N_{i-1}$ for
some $i$ with $0<i\leq t$. Hint: use part (c). 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
