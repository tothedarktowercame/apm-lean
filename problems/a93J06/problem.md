## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
If a function $f$ is analytic in the disk $D=\{z\pipe|z|<1\}$ and
obeys the conditions $f(0)=f'(0)=0$ and $|f'(z)|\leq 1$ for every
$z\in D$, show that $f$ satisfies the estimate $|f(z)|\leq |z|^2/2$
for every $z\in D$.
```
