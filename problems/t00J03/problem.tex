Let $x^0,x^1$ be standard coordinates on $\Rtw\setminus \{0\}$. Recall
that $\RP^1$ is the quotient space of $\Rtw\setminus \{0\}$ obtained
by identifying $(x^0,x^1)$ with $(\lambda x^0,\lambda x^1)$ for all
nonzero $\lambda$.  

The differential form $\frac{x^0 dx^1-x^1 dx^0}{(x^0)^2+(x^1)^2}$
decends to $\RP^1$, that is, there is a $1$-form $\omega$ on $\RP^1$
whose pullback to $\Rtw$ is the given form. Compute
$\int_{\RP^1}\omega$. Make sure to discuss orientation.
