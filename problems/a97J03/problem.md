## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $1<p<\infty$ and $1<q<\infty$ be such that $1/p+1/q=1$ and let
$f\in L^p(\R,m)$ and $g\in L^q(\R,m)$. Define $h:\R\rightarrow \C$ by
$h(y)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(y-x)g(x)dm(x)$. Prove that $h$ is
continuous and that $\lim_{y\rightarrow \pm\infty}h(y)=0$.
```
