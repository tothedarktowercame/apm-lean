## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f:[0,\infty)\rightarrow \R^+$ be a continuous function such that
$f(t)/t\rightarrow 1$ as $t\rightarrow 0$ and $t^4f(t)\rightarrow 1$
as $t\rightarrow \infty$. 

Consider the sequence of functions in $\Rn$ defined by
$F_k(x)=k^{\alpha}f(k|x|)$. Decide for every fixed $n$ for which
values of $\alpha$ 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $F_k\rightarrow 0$ uniformly;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $F_k\rightarrow 0$ a.e.;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $F_k\rightarrow 0$ in $L^1(\Rn)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
