## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $G$ be a finite group of order $pq$ where $p,q$ are prime and
$p<q$. Let $S_n$ denote the symmetric group on $n$ letters.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $G$ has a normal subgroup of order $q$. If $G$ has a
normal subgroup of order $p$, show that $G$ is abelian.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $G$ is nonabelian, show that $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup
of $S_q$. Show that $G$ is not isomorphic to a subgroup of $S_{q-1}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $G$ is abelian, show that $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of
$S_{p+q}$. Still assuming that $G$ is abelian, show that $G$ is not
isomorphic to a subgroup of $S_{p+q-1}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
