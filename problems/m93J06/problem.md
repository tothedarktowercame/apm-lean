## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item State and prove the theorem of existence and uniqueness for
solutions of the Cauchy problem $y'=f(x,y), y(0)=y_0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $f$ is $C^1$ then the map that takes $y_0$ to
$y(t)$ is $C^1$ for sufficiently small $t$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $|f(x,y)|\leq C|y|$, then the solution exists for
all $t$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $f$ is H\"older continuous with exponent $\alpha$,
the map that takes $y_0$ to $y(t)$ is H\"older continuous with
exponent $\alpha$ for sufficiently small $t$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What happens in parts (b) and (d) if $t$ is not small but
satisfies $|f(x,y)|\leq C|y|$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
