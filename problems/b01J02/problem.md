## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\Z[x]$ denote the ring of polynomials in one variable with
integer coefficients. If $R$ is a commutative ring with identity
element $1$, recall that an ideal $P$ of $R$ with $P\neq R$ is called
a prime ideal if $\forall a,b\in R: ab\in P \Rightarrow a\in P \vee b
\in P$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Describe all maximal ideals in $\Z[x]$ by giving their
generators and explain why your answer is correct.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that the ideal $(3,x^2+x+1)$ in $\Z[x]$ is not a prime ideal
by exhibiting $f(x), g(x)\in \Z[x]$ with $f(x)g(x)\in (3,x^2+x+1)$ but
$f(x) \notin (3,x^2+x+1)$ and $g(x) \notin (3,x^2+x+1)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Is every nonzero prime ideal in $\Z[x]$ a maximal ideal? If yes,
prove it. If no, give an example (with proof) of a nonzero prime ideal
which is not maximal.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
