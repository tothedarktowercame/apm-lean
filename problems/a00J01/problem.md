## Problem Statement

Assume that $f\in L^{\infty}([0,1],\mathcal{L},m)$ where $\mathcal{L}$
is the $\sigma$-algebra of Lebesgue measurable sets and $m$ is
Lebesgue measure. State the definition of $\|f\|_{\infty}$ and prove
that $\lim_{p\rightarrow \infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_{\infty}$.
