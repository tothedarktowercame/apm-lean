## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a PID and $F$ its field of fractions. Suppose $S$ is a ring
with $R\subset S\subset F$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that all elements $\alpha\in S$ can be written as $a/b$
where $a,b\in R$ and $1/b\in S$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $S$ is a PID.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $S$ is finitely generated as an $R$-module, then
$S=R$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
