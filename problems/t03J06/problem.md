## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\omega= z dx\wedge dy+(z^2 e^{xz}\ln(x^2+1)-y)dx\wedge dz+
(x+y^2z^3\cos (y^2+z^2))dy\wedge dz$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Is $\omega$ closed? Is $\omega$ exact?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $B$ be the bullet-shaped surface with boundary formed 
by taking the union of the cylinder $\{(x,y,z)\in \Rth \pipe
x^2+y^2=1, -1\leq z\leq 1\}$ with the hemisphere $\{(x,y,z)\in
\Rth\pipe x^2+y^2+(z-1)^1=1, z\geq 1\}$. Orient $B$ so that $dx\wedge
dy$ is positive at $(0,0,2)$. Compute $\int_B\omega$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\soln{ Compute $d\omega$. }
```
