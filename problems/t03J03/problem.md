## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be the connected sum of three projective planes.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What spaces are $2$-fold coverings of $X$? For each, describe
a covering map.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove the following or give a counterexample: Suppose $Y$ 
is orientable and $\pi:Y\rightarrow X$ is a $2$-fold covering map of
$X$. Then every embedding of a cylinder into $X$ lifts to $Y$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
