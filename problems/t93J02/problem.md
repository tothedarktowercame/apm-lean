## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $Y$ be a Hausdorff space. For $n=1,2,3,\ldots $ let $C_n\subset Y$
be a compact, connected set. Suppose $C_{n+1}\subset C_n$ for all
$n$. Show that $\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}C_n$ is a compact connected set.
```
