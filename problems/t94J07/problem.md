## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

Let $X$ and $Y$ be compact manifolds with $\partial X = \phi$. Let
$\omega$ be a closed form on $Y$. Let $f,g:X\rightarrow Y$ be smoothly
homotopic smooth maps. Prove that $\int_{X}f^{*}(\omega)=\int_X
g^*(\omega)$.
