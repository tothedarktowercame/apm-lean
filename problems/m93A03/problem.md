Assume $\{f_n\}$ is a sequence of vectors in a Hilbert space $F$ and
that $f\in F$ is such that $\langle f_n,g \rangle\rightarrow \langle
f,g \rangle$ for all $g\in F$. Assume further that $\|f_n\|$ converges
to $\|f\|$. Either prove that $\{f_n\}$ must converge to $f$ in norm
or give a counter example.
