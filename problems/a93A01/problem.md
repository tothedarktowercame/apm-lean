## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f:(0,1)\rightarrow \R$. Prove that the following are equivalent:
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $f$ is uniformly continuous
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $\forall \epsilon>0:\exists N>0:\forall x,y\in (0,1)$ with
$x\neq y$, if $|(f(x)-f(y))/(x-y)|>N$ then $|f(x)-f(y)|<\epsilon$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
