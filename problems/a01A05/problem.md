## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of real-valued functions in $L^2$ such
that $\|f_n\|_{L^2}=1$ and $$\forall n<m: |\int f_n f_m dx |\leq
2^{-m}.$$ (Which $\rightarrow 0$ as $m\rightarrow \infty$.) Let
$\{e_n\}$ be a Gram-Schmidt orthoginalization for this sequence with
$\|e_n\|=1$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $|f_m-e_m|\leq 2^{-m}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For any $g\in L^2$, show that as $m\rightarrow \infty$, $\int g
f_m dx \rightarrow 0$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
