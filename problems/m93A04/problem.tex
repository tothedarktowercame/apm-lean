Consider the map $F:C[0,1]\rightarrow C[0,1]$ defined by
$F(x)(t)=x(t)-\int_0^1K(t,x(s))ds$ where $K$ is a given function in
two real variables. Make an appropriate assumption about $K$ and show
that $F$ is differentiable. Give the formula for $F'$. Prove that $F'$
is invertable under further specific weak hypotheses.
