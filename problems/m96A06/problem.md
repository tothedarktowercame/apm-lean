## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $H=\{u\in L^2([0,1])\pipe u'\in L^2([0,1])\}$, in which $u'$
signifies the distributional derivative. In $H$ we use the norm
$\|u\|=[\int_0^1(|u(t)|^2+|u'(t)|^2)]^{1/2}$. Define $F:H\rightarrow
\R$ by the equation
$F(u)=\int_0^1(|u'(t)|^2+a(t)[u(t)]^2-2b(t)u(t))dt$ in which $a\in
L^{\infty}([0,1])$, $b\in L^2([0,1])$ and $a(t)\geq \epsilon>0$
a.e. on $[0,1]$. Compute the Fr\'echet derivative of $F$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that the equation $F'(u)=0$ is equivalent to a
Sturm-Liouville problem.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that there is a unique solution of the equation $F'(u)=0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
