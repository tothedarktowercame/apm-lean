Consider a real-valued nonnegative function $g\in L^1([0,1])$. Suppose
that $\int gf dx <A$ whenever $\int e^f dx \leq 1$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What can you say about the measure of the set $\{g>\lambda\}$
for large $\lambda$? (Try $f=c \chi_E$.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Can you say that $g\in L^2$? Why or why not?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
