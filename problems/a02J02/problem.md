## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of Lebesgue measurable functions in
$L^p([0,1])$ for some $p:1<p<\infty$. Assume that $f_n\rightarrow f$
almost everywhere  for $f\in L^p([0,1])$ with
$\|f_n\|_p\rightarrow \|f\|_p$. Show that $f_n\rightarrow f$ in
$L^p([0,1])$.
```
