Let $R$ be a Wedderburn ring (\ie\ an Artinian ring with $J(R)=0$) and
$M$ a right ideal. Suppose that $M$ is faithful as a module and no
subideal is faithful.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Explain why $M$ can be decomposed into a direct sum of simple
modules \ie\ $M\cong I_1\oplus\cdots\oplus I_k$ with each $I_i$ being
a simple module.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $J_i=\text{Ann}(I_i)$ (the annihilator of $I_i$), so $I_i$ is a
simple faithful $R/J_i$/module. Describe $R/J_i$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that the obvious ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\rightarrow
R/J_1\times \cdots \times R/J_k$ is an isomorphism.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $RM=R$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
