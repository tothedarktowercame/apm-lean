# APM b98J04: A minimal faithful right ideal recovers the Artin–Wedderburn decomposition

## Why It's Hard

A "Wedderburn ring" $R$ (Artinian with $J(R)=0$) is semisimple, so structurally it *is*
a product of matrix rings — but the problem asks you to **reconstruct** that product from
a single piece of data: a faithful right ideal $M$ with no faithful proper subideal. The
work is translating the two hypotheses correctly: *faithful* means $M$ meets every simple
block ($\bigcap\operatorname{Ann}(I_i)=0$), and *minimal faithful* means it meets each
block exactly once (the simple summands are pairwise non-isomorphic). Only then do the
annihilators become the distinct maximal two-sided ideals whose CRT product is $R$.

## The Key Insight

- **(a)** $R$ semisimple $\Rightarrow R_R$ semisimple $\Rightarrow$ the submodule $M$ is a
  finite direct sum of simple modules.
- **(b)** $J_i=\operatorname{Ann}(I_i)$ is two-sided; $R/J_i$ is semisimple with a
  faithful *simple* module, hence a **simple Artinian ring** $\cong M_{n_i}(D_i)$.
- **(c)** $\ker\varphi=\bigcap J_i=\operatorname{Ann}(M)=0$ (faithful). Minimality forces
  the $I_i$ pairwise non-isomorphic, so the $J_i$ are *distinct maximal* two-sided ideals,
  hence pairwise comaximal; CRT makes $\varphi$ onto. So $\varphi$ is an isomorphism.
- **(d)** $M$ contains a copy of every simple, and the two-sided ideal generated by a
  simple is its whole matrix block, so $RM$ contains every block: $RM=R$.

## Complete Proof

$R$ is Artinian with $J(R)=0$, i.e. **semisimple**; $M$ is a faithful right ideal with no
faithful proper subideal.

### (a) $M$ is a direct sum of simple modules

Since $J(R)=0$ and $R$ is Artinian, $R$ is semisimple, so the right regular module $R_R$
is a direct sum of simple right ideals. A submodule of a semisimple module is semisimple;
$M$ is a submodule of $R_R$, hence semisimple, i.e. a direct sum of simple modules. As
$R$ is Artinian and $M\subseteq R$ is finitely generated, the sum is finite:
$$
M\cong I_1\oplus\cdots\oplus I_k,\qquad I_i\ \text{simple}. \qquad\checkmark
$$

### (b) $R/J_i$ is a simple Artinian ring $M_{n_i}(D_i)$

$J_i=\operatorname{Ann}(I_i)=\{r\in R: I_i r=0\}$ is the kernel of the ring map $R\to
\operatorname{End}(I_i)$ given by the action, hence a **two-sided** ideal, and $I_i$ is a
*faithful* simple module over $R/J_i$. Now $R/J_i$ is a quotient of the semisimple ring
$R$, hence semisimple, so by Artin–Wedderburn $R/J_i\cong\prod_j M_{m_j}(D_j)$. A faithful
simple module over a product forces a single factor: if $R/J_i=A\times B$ with both
nonzero, a simple module is a module over exactly one factor (the other acting as $0$),
contradicting faithfulness unless the other factor is $0$. Hence
$$
R/J_i\cong M_{n_i}(D_i)
$$
for a division ring $D_i$ — a **simple Artinian ring**, and $I_i$ is its unique simple
module. In particular $J_i$ is a maximal two-sided ideal of $R$ (its quotient is simple).
$\quad\checkmark$

### (c) $\varphi:R\to R/J_1\times\cdots\times R/J_k$ is an isomorphism

The map $\varphi(r)=(r+J_1,\dots,r+J_k)$ is a ring homomorphism (product of quotient
maps).

**Injective.** $\ker\varphi=\bigcap_i J_i=\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}(I_i)=
\operatorname{Ann}\!\big(\bigoplus_i I_i\big)=\operatorname{Ann}(M)=0$, since $M$ is
faithful.

**The summands are pairwise non-isomorphic.** Suppose $I_i\cong I_j$ as $R$-modules for
some $i\neq j$ (equivalently $J_i=J_j$). Drop $I_j$: the subideal $M'=\bigoplus_{l\neq j}
I_l$ still contains a module isomorphic to $I_j$ (namely $I_i$), so
$\operatorname{Ann}(M')=\bigcap_{l\neq j}\operatorname{Ann}(I_l)=\bigcap_l
\operatorname{Ann}(I_l)=0$ (removing the duplicate annihilator changes nothing). Thus $M'$
is a faithful proper subideal — contradicting minimality. Hence the $I_i$ are pairwise
non-isomorphic, so the $J_i$ are **distinct** maximal two-sided ideals.

**Surjective.** Distinct maximal two-sided ideals are pairwise comaximal: for $i\neq j$,
$J_i+J_j$ properly contains the maximal ideal $J_i$, so $J_i+J_j=R$. By the Chinese
Remainder Theorem for pairwise comaximal (two-sided) ideals, $\varphi$ is surjective with
kernel $\bigcap J_i=0$.

Therefore $\varphi$ is a ring isomorphism
$$
R\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ R/J_1\times\cdots\times R/J_k\ \cong\ \prod_{i=1}^k M_{n_i}(D_i),
$$
which is exactly the Artin–Wedderburn decomposition of $R$. $\quad\blacksquare$

### (d) $RM=R$

$RM=\{\sum_l r_l m_l: r_l\in R,\ m_l\in M\}$ is the two-sided ideal generated by $M$ (it
is a left ideal containing the right ideal $M$, hence two-sided). Identify $R\cong\prod_i
A_i$ with $A_i=R/J_i\cong M_{n_i}(D_i)$ via (c); under this, $I_i$ is a simple module
living in the block $A_i$ (every other block annihilates $I_i$).

For each $i$, the set $RI_i$ is a two-sided ideal of $R$ contained in the block $A_i$
(since $A_i$ is a two-sided ideal and $I_i\subseteq A_i$), and it is nonzero (it contains
$I_i\neq0$). But $A_i$ is a *simple* ring, so it has no two-sided ideals other than $0$
and $A_i$; hence $RI_i=A_i$. Therefore
$$
RM=\sum_{i=1}^k RI_i=\sum_{i=1}^k A_i=\prod_{i=1}^k A_i=R .
$$
So $RM=R$. $\quad\blacksquare$
(Equivalently: $1\in R=\bigoplus A_i$ and each $A_i=RI_i\subseteq RM$, so $1\in RM$.)

### Remark

The two adjectives on $M$ do precisely complementary jobs: **faithful** $=$ "$M$ touches
every Wedderburn block" ($\bigcap J_i=0$), and **minimal faithful** $=$ "$M$ touches each
block only once" (the $I_i$ are pairwise non-isomorphic). Together they make $M$ a
"transversal" of the simple modules, so its annihilators $J_i$ are exactly the $k$ maximal
two-sided ideals, one per block, and $R\cong\prod R/J_i$ reconstructs the whole ring. A
minimal faithful module is thus the module-theoretic fingerprint of the Artin–Wedderburn
decomposition, and (d) records that such an $M$ generates $R$ as a two-sided ideal.
