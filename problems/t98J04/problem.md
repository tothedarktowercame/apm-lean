# t98J04

## Bundle Overview

This directory is the canonical APM problem bundle for this problem.

## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source is preserved verbatim in `problem.tex`:

```tex
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $c_1$ and $c_2$ be two interlinked circles in $\Rth$.
Show that $c_2$ is not homotopically trivial in $\Rth \setminus c_1$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $k$ be a simple closed curve in the solid torus $T$ made by
grabing a loop, pushing it into a small region inside the torus, then
then stretching it around the hole in both directions and then causing
the two ends to interlink. Show that $k$ does not bound a disk in
$T$. Hint: look at the universal covering space of $T$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
