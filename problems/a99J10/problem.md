## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f$ be holomorphic on the open unit disk
$\Delta=\{z\in\C \pipe |z|<1\}$.  Assume that $|f(x+iy)|\leq |y|^{-1/2}$ at
all points $z\in \Delta$ and that $\lim_{r\rightarrow
1-}f(re^{i\theta})=0$ for any $\theta\in [0,2\pi]$. Prove that $f$ is
identically $0$ on $\Delta$.
```
