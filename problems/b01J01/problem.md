## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $|S|$ denote the cardinality of a finite set $S$. Let $G$ be a
finite group and let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Suppose $K$ is a normal
subgroup of $G$. Let $G'$ be the commutator subgroup of $G$. Let
$Sp(G)$ (resp. $Sp(K)$) be the Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$ (resp. $K$).
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose $p$ is a prime number that divides $|H|$ but does not
divide $|H\cap K|$. Show that $p$ divides $|G/K|$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item  Suppose $p$ is a
prime number which divides $|H|$ but does not divide $|H\cap
G'|$. Show that $G$ has a subgroup of index $p$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $Sp(K)=\{P\cap K \pipe  P\in Sp(G)\}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that  $|Sp(K)|$ divides $|Sp(G)|$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
