## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Assume that $(X,\mathcal{A},\lambda)$ is a measure space and $f\in
L^1((X,\mathcal{A},\lambda))$, with
$\|f\|_1=\int_X|f(x)|d\lambda(x)=1$. Prove that $\int_E\log |f(x)|
d\lambda(x)\leq -\lambda(E)\log \lambda(E)$ for all subsets $E\in
\mathcal{A}$ such that $0<\lambda(E)<\infty$.
```
