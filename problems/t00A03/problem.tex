Let $F$ be a a closed orientable surface of genus $g\geq 2$. Recall
that a closed curve $c$ on $F$ is \emph{simple} if it has no
self-intersections and is \emph{essential} if it does not bound a disk
in $F$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that a maximal collection of essential, nonparallel
pairwise disjoint simple closed curves on $F$ cut $F$ into a
collection of three-times-punctured spheres. (A pair of curves is
\emph{nonparallel} if they do not co-bound an annulus in $F$.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that this maximal number is $3g-g$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
