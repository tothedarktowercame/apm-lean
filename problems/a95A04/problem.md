## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
A Lebesgue integrable function $f:[0,1]\rightarrow \R$ has the
property that $\int_{x-h}^x f(t)dt=\int_x^{x+h}f(t)dt$ whenever $x\in
[0,1]$, and $h>0$ are such that $[x-h,x+h]$ is contained in
$[0,1]$. Prove that $f(x)=\int_0^1f(t)dt$ for almost every $x\in
[0,1]$.  Hint: compute $\int_If(t)dt$ for $I=[k2^{-j},(k+1)2^{-j}]$
where $j=1,2,3,\ldots $, and $k$ is an integer,  $k\in [0,2^j-1]$.
```
