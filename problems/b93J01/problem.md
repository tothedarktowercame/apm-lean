## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Show these statements are false and correct them.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item The alternating group $A_n$ is simple (\ie\ has no nontrivial
normal subgroups) for every positive integer $n\geq 3$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Each group of order $8$ is isomorphic to $\Z/8\Z, \Z/4\Z\oplus
\Z/2\Z$ or the dihdral group $D_4$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $G$ is a group of order $n$ and if $m$ is any positive divisor
of $n$ then $G$ has a subgroup of order $n$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Every Abelian group is isomorphic to a direct sum of cyclic
groups.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $q$ is an odd prime and $p$ is any prime less than $q$ then
any group of order $pq$ is cyclic.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
