## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f:[0,1]\rightarrow\R$ be absolutely continuous and satisfy
$f(0)=0$ and also $\int_0^1|f'(x)|^2 dm(x)<\infty$. Show that
$\lim_{x\rightarrow 0+}x^{-1/2}f(x)$ exists and determine the value of
the limit.
```
