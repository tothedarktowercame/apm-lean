## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Identify the $3$-sphere $\Sth$ as the set of points
$\{(z_1,z_2)\in\C^2 \pipe |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}$. Identify the $2$-sphere with
the complex projective line $\CP^1$ consisting of pairs of complex
numbers $(z_1,z_2)$ not both zero with $[\lambda z_1,\lambda
z_2]=[z_1,z_2]$ for $\lambda\in \C\setminus \{0\}$. Define
$\pi:\Sth\rightarrow \Stw$ by $\pi((z_1,z_2))=[z_1,z_2]$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $\pi$ is a submersion.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Identify the manifold $\pi^{-1}([1,0])$. Can you identify
$\pi^{-1}(p)$ for all $p\in \Stw$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Does there exist a continuous map $\iota:\Stw\rightarrow \Stw$
so that $\pi\circ \iota$ is the identity map of $\Stw$? Construct or
prove the nonexistence.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
