Let $\{x_n\}^{\infty}_{n=1}$ be a sequence in the Hilbert space $H$
and suppose that it has the weak limit $x_n \rightharpoonup x$ in
$H$. Show that
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item The sequence $\{x_n\}^{\infty}_{n=1}$ is bounded in $H$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $x_n\rightarrow x \Leftrightarrow \lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}
\|x_n\|=\|x\|$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $\exists \{\{\alpha_i, \alpha_i>0\}_{i=1}^n\}_{n=1}^{\infty} :
\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i^n=1, \lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\sum_{i=1}^n
\alpha_i^nx_i=x$ (strong limit).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
