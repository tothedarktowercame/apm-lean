Let $G$ be a group of order $10$. Let $P$ be a Sylow $5$-subgroup of
$G$ and let $Q$ be a Sylow $2$-subgroup of $G$. Let $P$ be generated
by $x$ and let $Q$ be generated by $y$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that the automorphism group of $P$, $\mathrm{Aut}(P)$
is $\{\Psi_1,\Psi_2,\Psi_3,\Psi_4\}$ where for $1\leq n\leq 4$
$\Psi_n(x^i)=x^{ni}$ for $0\leq i\leq 4$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there are exactly two homomorphisms from $Q$ to
$\mathrm{Aut}(P)$. Specify exactly what they are by saying in each
case where $y$ is mapped. (Recall that $y$ is a generator for $Q$.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $G$ is isomorphic to some semi-direct product of $P$
by $Q$. (There is a theorem that says if certain hypotheses hold for
$P$ and $Q$ then $G$ is isomorphic to a semi-direct product of $P$ by
$Q$. State that theorem and prove that those hypotheses on $P$ and $Q$
hold here. You do not have to prove the theorem itself.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For the rest of the problem, suppose $G$ is \emph{not} Abelian.

Since $G=PQ$ and since $x$ generates $P$ and $y$ generates $q$, we
see that $G=\{1,x^2,x^3,x^4,y,xy,x^2y,x^3y,x^4y\}.$

Which of these ten elements is $yx$ equal to? Justify your answer
using the theory of semi-direct products.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Which of the above ten elements does $(x^2y)(x^3y)$ equal.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
