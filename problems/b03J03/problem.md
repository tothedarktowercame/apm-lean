## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $D$ be a unique factorization domain and let $K$ be its field of
fractions.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $D$ is integrally closed in $K$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove or disprove: if $a$ is a square-free element of $D$, \ie\
$a$ is not divisible in $D$ by the square of a prime element, then
$D[x]/(x^2-a)$ is integrally closed in $K[x]/(x^2-a)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
