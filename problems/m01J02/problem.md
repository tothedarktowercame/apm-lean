## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space, and $M$ and $N$ closed subspaces. Let
$P_M$ and $P_N$ be the orthogonal projection operators from $H$ onto
$M$ and $N$ respectively.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define what it means to say that $P_M$ is an \emph{orthogonal
projection}.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $P_M+P_N=P_{M+N}$ if and only if $M\perp N$. Here
$P_{M+N}$ is the orthogonal projection onto $M+N$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $P_MP_N$ is an orthogonal projection if and only if
$P_MP_N=P_NP_M$. In this case, show that $P_MP_N$ is orthogonal
projection onto $P\cap N$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
