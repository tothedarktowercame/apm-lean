## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\Ttw=\Rtw/\Z^2$ and let $\hat{f}:\Rtw\rightarrow \Rtw$ be the map
$\hat{f}(x,y)=(\sin(2\pi x + 4\pi y),x+y)$. Since
$\hat{f}(x+n,y+m)=\hat{f}(x,y)$ for any integers $n$ and $m$,
$\hat{f}$ decends to a map $f:\Ttw\rightarrow \Ttw$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute the degree of $f$. Hint: think homotopy.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show (by any means you wish) that $f$ is not homotopic to a
constant map.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
