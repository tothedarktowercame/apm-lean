## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\gamma$ be a piecewise smooth differentiable path,
$\gamma:[0,1]\rightarrow \C$ with $\gamma(0)=\pi$ and
$\gamma^{-1}([0,\infty))=\{0\}$. Prove that $0< | \Im
\{\int_{\gamma} \frac{dz}{z} \}| < 2 \pi$.
```
