Let $\Sigma$ be a smooth surface of genus two and let
$f:\Sigma\rightarrow \Sigma$ be a smooth map. Let $C\subset \Sigma$ be
a smooth curve wrapping around one of the handles that make up
$\Sigma$. Suppose that $f$ is transverse to $C$. Prove that if every
component of $f^{-1}(C)$ bounds an embedded disk in $\Sigma$, then the
degree of $f$ is zero.
