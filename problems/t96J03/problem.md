## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a non-empty closed subset of $\Rn$ and let $y_0\in
\Rn\setminus X$. Show that there exists $x_0\in X$ such that
$\|x_0-y_0\|\leq \|x-y_0\|$ for all $x\in X$.
```
