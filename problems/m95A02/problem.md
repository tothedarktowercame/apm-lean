## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Consider the Hilbert space $H=W^{1,2}$ of complex functions on
$(-\infty,\infty)$ that have period $2\pi$, with the inner product
defined by $\langle f,g
\rangle_H=\int_{0}^{2\pi}f'(x)\bar{g'(x)}+f(x)\bar{g(x)}dx$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Use Fourier series to find an orthonormal basis for $H$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that for all $f\in H:|f(x)-f(y)|\leq
|x-y|^{1/2}\|f\|_{H}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Use the Arzela-Ascoli theorem and (b) to prove that every weakly
convergent sequence in $H$ converges in the maximum-norm topology.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
