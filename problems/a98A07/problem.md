## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $(f_n(z))$ be a sequence of functions holomorphic on an open
region containing the closed unit disk satisfying the following:
$|f_n(e^{it})|\leq 1/(t(\log(t/4\pi))^2)$ for all $0<t<2\pi$ and
$\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}f_n(e^{it})$ exists a.e. for $0<t<2\pi$.

Prove that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly on compact subsets of the open
unit disk to a holomorphic function $f$. Hint: let
$g(t)=\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}f_n(e^{it})$. Attempt to define $f$
using the Cauchy integral formula thinking of $f(e^{it})=g(t)$.
```
