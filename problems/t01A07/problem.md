## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X,Y,Z,W$ be homogenous coordinate on $\RP^3$. Consider the
surface $S\subset \RP^3$ defined by the homogeneous equation
$X^4-Y^4-Z^4+W^4=0$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $S$ is a smooth submanifold of $\RP^3$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Fix a projective line $\ell\subset \RP^3$, for example
$\ell=\{[X,Y,Z,W]\in \RP^3 \pipe  Z=W=0\}$. Compute the intersection number
$I_{\RP^3}(S,\ell)$. You may assume that $S$ and $\ell$ are orientable
(they are) and compute the intersection number up to a sign which
depends on globally changing coordinates.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
