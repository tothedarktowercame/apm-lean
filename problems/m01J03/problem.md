## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $T:H\rightarrow H$ a compact operator
such that $\|T\|=1$. Show that $(T^*T)^n$ converges for $n\rightarrow
\infty$ to the operator $P_A$, the orthogonal projection onto a finite
dimensional subspace $A$.
```
