## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of functions that are both analytic and
free of zeros in a planar domain $D$. Assuming that $f_n\rightarrow f$
uniformly on compact subsets of $D$, demonstrate that $f$ either has
no zeros in $D$ or vanishes identically there.
```
