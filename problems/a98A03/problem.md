## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ and assume that $f'(x)$ exists almost
everywhere with $\esssup_{0\leq x\leq 1}(f'(x))<\infty$. Consider the
equation $f(1)-f(0)=\int_0^1 f'(t) dt$. Does this equation hold in
general? Does it hold if we assume that in addition $f'(x)$ exists for
all $x$ such that $0<x<1$ and $f'$ is Riemann integrable? Hint: the
intermediate value theorem is relevant for the second question.
```
