## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space over an algebraically
closed field and let $A$ be a family of commuting linear
transformations of $V$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there is a vector $v\in V$ that is an eigenvector
for every $a\in A$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there is an ordered basis $v_1,v_2,\ldots v_n$ for $V$
with respect to which the matrix for every $a\in A$ is triangular.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
