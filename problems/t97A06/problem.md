## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a nonempty compact oriented smooth submanifold of the
manifold $Y$ with $\dim X>0$. Let $\eta$ be the volume form on
$X$. Let $i:X\rightarrow Y$ be the inclusion map.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there exists a form $\omega$ on $Y$ such that
$i^*(\omega)=\eta$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $i$ is homotopic to a constant map, show that $\omega$ is not
a closed form.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
