## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be the unit sphere in $\mathbf{A}^4$ which in standard affine
coordinates $x^1,x^2,x^3,x^4$ is the set of solutions to the equation
$(x^2)^2+(x^2)^2+(x^3)^2+(x^4)^2=1$. Consider $Z\subset X$ defined by
the equation $x^3x^4=1/4$.  
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $Z$ is a submanifold of $X$. What is $\mathrm{dim}Z$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $\Stw$ be the unit $2$-sphere defined by the equation 
$(y^1)^2+(y^2)^2+(y^3)^2=1$ in an affine space with standard
coordinates $y^1,y^2,y^3$. Prove that there exists a dense subset of
$\{(\lambda,\mu) \pipe \lambda^2+\mu^2=1\}$ such that the map
$\Stw\rightarrow X, (y^1,y^2,y^3)\mapsto
(y^1\lambda,\mu,y^2\lambda,y^3\lambda)$ is transverse to $Z$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
