## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $G$ be a group of order $36$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $G$ does not contain a normal subgroup of order
$9$, then $G$ contains a normal subgroup $K$ of order $3$ and
$G/K\cong A_4$. (You may use without proof the fact that $A_4$ is the
only subgroup of $S_4$ with order $12$.) For the rest of the problem,
assume such a subgroup $K$ does exist.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $A_4$ has a normal subgroup of order $4$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show tha $G$ has a normal subgroup $N$ of order $12$ containing
$K$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that either $N$ is abelian or $|Z(N)|=2$. (Here $Z(N)$
denotes the center.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $Z(N)<G$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Remark: Since part (e) is incompatible with $|Z(N)|=2$, you have
proved that every group of order $36$ is the semi-direct product of
Sylow subgroups.
```
