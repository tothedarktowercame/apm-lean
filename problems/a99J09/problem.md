## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Assume that $f:[0,\infty)\rightarrow \C$ belongs to
$L^p([0,\infty),m)$ where $1<p<\infty$ and $m$ denotes Lebesgue
measure. Define $F:\{z\in \C \pipe 0<\Re(z)\}\rightarrow \C$ by
$F(z)=\int_0^{\infty}f(t)\exp \{-tz\} dm(t)$. Prove that $F$ is
holomorphic on $\{z\in \C \pipe 0<\Re(z)\}$.
```
