## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $c\in \R$ and $F(\cdot )\in L^2((0,1))$ be given.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Discuss the solvability of the boundary value problem
$-u''(x)+cu(x)=F(x)$ ($x\in (0,1)$) and $u(0)=0$, $u'(1)=0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $G:\R\rightarrow \R$ be a Lipschitz monotone
function, and let $g$ be an antiderivative of $G$, \ie\ $g'(x)=G(x)$
for all $x\in R$, and suppose that $g(0)=0$. Show that the functional
$J(u)=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 u'(x)^2
dx+\int_0^1g(u(x))dx-\int_0^1F(x)u(x)dx$ is Fr\'echet differentiable
on $H^1((0,1))$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there is a unique solution to the boundary value
problem $-u''(x)+G(u(x))=F(x)$ ($x\in (0,1)$) and $u(0)=0$, $u'(1)=0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
