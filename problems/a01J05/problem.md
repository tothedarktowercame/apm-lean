## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f(z)$ be analytic on an open set that contains
$\bar{\Delta}=\{\omega\in \C \pipe |\omega|\leq 1\}$. Assume that
$|f(z)|=1$ if $z$ is on the boundary of $\bar{\Delta}$ and that $f$
has $N$ zeros in the interior of $\bar{\Delta}$. Prove that $f'(z)$
has $N-1$ zeros in the interior of $\bar{\Delta}$. Here all zeros are
counted with multiplicity.
```
