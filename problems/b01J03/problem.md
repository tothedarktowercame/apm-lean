## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f(x)$ be a separable irreducible monic polynomial over a field
$F$ and let $K$ be the splitting field of $f(x)$ over $F$. Suppose
$\mathrm{deg}(f(x))=p$ where $p$ is a prime number. Let $G$ be the
Galois group of $K$ over $F$. Let $P$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup of
$G$. Let $N$ be the normalizer of $P$ in $G$. Suppose $P=N$; the goal
is to show that $P=G$. Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be roots of $f(x)$ in
$K$ and let $H_{\alpha}$ be the Galois group of $K$ over $F[\alpha]$
 $H_{\beta}$ be the Galois group of $K$ over $F[\beta]$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $|G|=ps$ with $s$ an integer not divisible by $p$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item With $s$ as in part (a), show that there are exactly $s(p-1)$
elements of order $p$ in $G$. Hint: how many conjugates does $P$ have
in $G$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item  Show that $H_{\alpha}=H_{\beta}$ and $F[\beta]=F[\alpha]$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $K=F[\alpha]$ and conclude that $P=G$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
