## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
The Laplacian operator in $2$ variables is $\triangle:u\mapsto
u_{xx}+u_{yy} $. This operator is often approximated by
$Tu=[u(x+h,y)+u(x-h,y)+u(x,y+h)+u(x,y-h)-4u(x,y)]/h^2$. Prove that
functions whose derivatives $u_{xxxx}$ and $u_{yyyy}$ are continuous
satisfy an error bound of the form $|(\triangle u)(x,y)-(Tu)(x,y)\leq
Ch^2|$.
```
