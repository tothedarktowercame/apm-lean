## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $C$ denote the standard Cantor set. Let $X=\{x\pipe x \text{\ is
irrational\ }, 0<x<1\}$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Does there exist a continuous surjection $f:C\rightarrow X$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Does there exist a continuous injection $f:C\rightarrow X$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\soln{The Cantor set is what you get when you remove the middle third of
every interval... The topology should be the ``subspace topology''.  I
guess the topology on $X$ is the same, the subspace topology.}
```
