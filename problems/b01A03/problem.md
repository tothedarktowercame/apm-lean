## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a principle ideal domain, $M$ a finitely generated free
$R$-module, and $S\subset M$ a submodule.  Show that the following are
equivalent:
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item The submodule $S$ is \emph{complemented}, \ie\ there is a
submodule $T\subset M$ with $M\cong S \oplus T$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item The quotient module $M/S$ is a free $R$-module.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $x\in S$ and $x=ay$ for some $y\in M$ and $a\neq 0\in R$ then
$y\in S$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
