## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $K\leq \R$ be a compact set with positive Lebesgue measure. Define
$f:(\C\setminus K)\rightarrow \C$ by $f(z)=\int_K\frac{1}{t-z}dm(t)$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $f$ is holomorphic on $\C\setminus K$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that $f$ cannot be extended by analytic continuation to an
entire function.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $\lim_{|z|\rightarrow \infty}zf(z)$ exists and
determine its value.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
