## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $A\subset [0,1]$ be a closed set. For each positive integer $N$
let $b(n)$ be the number of integers $n$ such that $1\leq n\leq N$ and
$[(n-1)/N,n/N]\cap A\neq \phi$. Prove that $\lim_{N\rightarrow
\infty}b(N)/N=m(A)$, where $m$ denotes Lebesgue measure.
```
