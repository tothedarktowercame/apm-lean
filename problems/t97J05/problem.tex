Consider the map $f:\Rth\rightarrow \Rtw$ defined by
$f(x,y,z)=(e^x\cos(y),xz)$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute $df_p$ at the point $p=(1,\pi/2,1)$. Is $p$ a regular
point of $f$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $W=f^{-1}(0,1)$. is $W$ a manifold? If so, find an
\emph{explicit} parameterization of $W$ near $p$. If not, find an
\emph{explicit} point where $W$ fails to be a manifold.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Do there exist diffeomorphisms $\varphi$ and $\psi$ such that 
$\varphi$ is defined as a map from a neighborhood of the origin in
$\Rtw$ to a neighborhood of $p$ and $\psi$ is defined as a map from a
neighborhood of $(0,1)\in \Rtw$ to a neighborhood of the origin in
$\Rtw$ with the property that $\psi\circ f\circ \varphi$ is given by
the explicit formula $\psi\circ f\circ \varphi(x,y,z)=(x,y)$? Why or
why not?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
