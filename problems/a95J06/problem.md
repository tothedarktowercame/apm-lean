## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $C\subset [0,1]$ be the Cantor set and suppose that
$f:[0,1]\rightarrow [0,1]$ is a homeomorphism such that $f(x)=x$ for
all $x\in C$. If $f$ is absolutely continuous on every closed interval
in $[0,1]\setminus C$, prove that $f$ is absolutely continuous on
$[0,1]$.
```
