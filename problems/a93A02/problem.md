## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subset L^2([0,1])$. Assume
$\sup_n\|f_n\|_2<\infty$ and let $1\leq p<2$. Let $f:[0,1]\rightarrow
\R$ and assume that $f_n\rightarrow f$ almost everywhere. Prove that
$\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\|f_n-f\|_p=0$. Give an example to show
that this is false for $p=2$.
```
