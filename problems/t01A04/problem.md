## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be the union of a circle $\Son$ and a $2$-torus $\Son\times
\Son$, identified along a point $x$. 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find a presentation for $\pi_1(X,x)$. Identify the generators 
of this presentation in a picture of $X$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item In terms of the generators in part (a), describe two index 
two subgroups of $\pi_1(X,x)$ that correspond to nonisomorphic
covering spaces.  Sketch the corresponding covering spaces.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
```
