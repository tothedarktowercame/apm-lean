# t98A06

## Bundle Overview

This directory is the canonical APM problem bundle for this problem.

## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source is preserved verbatim in `problem.tex`:

```tex
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $f:X\rightarrow Y$ be a submersion and let $\mathbf{v}$ be a
vector field on $Y$. Prove that there exists a vector field
$\mathbf{w}$ on $X$ such that for all $x\in X$,
$df_x\mathbf{w}(x)=\mathbf{v}(f(x))$. Hint: partition of unity.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $X$ be a compact manifold without boundary and
$f:X\rightarrow \Son$ a submersion. Prove that $f^{-1}(1,0)$ and
$f^{-1}(-1,0)$ are diffeomorphic. Hint: flows.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
