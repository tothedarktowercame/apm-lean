## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $\S^k$ denote the $k$-sphere of radius $k$ in $\R^{k+1}$ and let
$X$ denote the product $\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}\S^k$ in the product
topology. Suppose $f:X\rightarrow \Rth$ is a continuous map. Is it
always possible to find a sphere centered at the origin in $\Rth$ which 
is disjoint from $f(X)$?
```
