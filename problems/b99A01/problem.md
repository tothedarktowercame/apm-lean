## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $H=\{z\pipe \Im z>0\}$. Suppose $f:\bar{H}\rightarrow \C$ is
bounded and continuous and that $f|_H$ is analytic. Assume that
$\forall x\in \R:|f(z)|\leq M<\infty$. Show that $|f(z)|\leq M$ for
all $z\in H$.
Assume that $V$ is a commutative integral domain with quotient field
$F$ and that $V$ has the property that $\forall \alpha\in F:\alpha\neq
0\Rightarrow (\alpha\in V \vee \alpha^{-1}\in V)$. (Such a $V$ is
called a \emph{valuation domain}.)
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $I,J$ be ideals of $V$. Show that either $I\subset J$ or
$J\subset I$. Note that this implies that $V$ has a unique maximal
ideal.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $I$ is a finitely generated ideal of $V$. Show that
$I$ is a principal ideal. Hint: consider ideals with two-element
generating sets.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $\alpha\in F$, $\alpha\neq 0$ and suppose that there exist
$a_0,a_1,\ldots ,a_{n-1}\in V$ such that
$\alpha^n+a_{n-1}\alpha^{n-1}+\ldots +a_0\in V$. Show that $\alpha\in
V$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $W$ be an integral domain with $V\subset W\subset F$. Show
that $W$ is also a valuation domain.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $M$ be the unique maximal ideal of $W$. Show that
$W=\{a/s\pipe a\in V,s\in V\setminus M\}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
