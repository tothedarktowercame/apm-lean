Consider the map $f:\Stw\rightarrow \R^6$ defined by
$f(x_0,x_1,x_2)=(x_0^2,x_1^2,x_2^2,x_0x_1,x_0x_2,x_1x_2)$ where $\Stw$
is the unit sphere centered at the origin (in $\Rth$). Since
$f(-x_0,-x_1,-x_2)=f(x_0,x_1,x_2)$, $f$ induces a map
$\tilde{f}:\RP^2\rightarrow \R^6$. Show that $\tilde{f}$ is a smooth
embedding onto a submanifold of $\R^6$.
