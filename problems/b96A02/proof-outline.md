## Remaining boundaries

1. Formalize the order-30 group classification.  Mathlib proves squarefree
   groups are Z-groups and classifies finite Z-groups as semidirect products of
   cyclic coprime factors, but does not extract the required normal subgroup of
   order 15 or normality of all subgroups of orders 3 and 5.  The compiled
   helper `apm_b96a02_normal_of_card_fifteen` now proves that every subgroup of
   order 15 is normal, by computing its index as two.  The helper
   `apm_b96a02_normalCore_eq_bot_or_eq_self_of_prime_card` additionally proves
   that the normal core of every prime-order subgroup is either bottom or the
   subgroup itself, using the prime-cardinality subgroup dichotomy after
   restricting the core to the subgroup.  Constructing the order-15 subgroup
   and excluding the bottom-core branch for the order-3 and order-5 subgroups
   remain open.
2. Prove that the fixing subgroup of the normal closure of an intermediate
   field is its normal core, then identify the normal closure of
   `F(alpha)` with the field generated by all roots of `minpoly F alpha`.
   The former follows from existing Galois-connection APIs; the latter
   simple-extension specialization is not packaged.
3. Combine those results with fixing-subgroup cardinality/finrank formulas to
   classify core-free subgroups of a group of order 30 and obtain degree 15 or
   30.
