Consider the minimization of the intergral $\int_a^b F(x,y(x),y'(x))
dx$ by choice of the real function $y$ subject to the constraints
$y(a)=\alpha$ and $y(b)=\beta$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Derive the Euler equation for this problem.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Use a suitable function $F$ and the Euler equation to prove that
the shortest distance between two points in the plane is along a
straight line.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
