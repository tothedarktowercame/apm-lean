## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $A,B,C,D$ be $n\times n$
matrices over a field $K$. Let $\mathrm{tr}(A)$ denote the trace of
$A$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $A^m=0$ for some positive integer $m$, show that
$\mathrm{tr}(A)=0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $\mathrm{tr}(BC)=0$ for all $n\times n$ matrices $B$ over
$K$, show that $C=0$ (\ie\ the $n\times n$ zero matrix).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $D^2=D$, show that $\mathrm{tr}(D)=\mathrm{rank}(D)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
