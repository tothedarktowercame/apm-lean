## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Consider the functional $I(y)=\int_{0}^a[(y'(x))^2-(y(x))^2]dx$, where
$a>0$ and $y\in C^1([0,1])$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find the extrema of $I(y)$ when $a=2\pi$ and $y(0)=y(2\pi)=1$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find the extrema of $I(y)$ when $a$ is \emph{not} a multiple of
$\pi$, if $y(0)=0$ and $y(a)=1$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find the extrema of $I(y)$ subject to the constraint
$\int_0^{\pi}(y(x))^2dx=1$ when $a=\pi$ and $y(0)=y(\pi)=0$. What is
the best constant in the Poincar\'e inequality $\int_{0}^{\pi}f^2
dx\leq C \int_0^{\pi}f'^2 dx$ for $f\in H_0^1(0,\pi)$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
