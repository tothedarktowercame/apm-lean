## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Suppose $\pi:\tilde{X}\rightarrow X$ is a $k$-sheeted covering map
where $X,\tilde{X}$ are compact oriented manifolds of dimension $n$
and $\pi$ is orientation-preserving.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Construct an example of such a covering map for surfaces ($n=2$)
for any $k$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For any $k$-sheeted covering $\tilde{X}\rightarrow X$ of compact
oriented $n$-manifolds and any $\omega\in \Omega^n(X)$, show that
$\int_{\tilde{X}}\pi^*\omega=k\int_X \omega$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $Z_1,Z_2\subset X$ are compact oriented
submanifolds with $\mathrm{dim}(Z_1)+\mathrm{dim}(Z_2)=n$.  Prove that
$\tilde{Z}_i=\pi^{-1}(Z_i)$ are compact oriented submanifolds of
$\tilde{X}$ and the intersection number satisfies
$I_{\tilde{X}}(\tilde{Z}_1,\tilde{Z}_2)=k I_X(Z_1,Z_2)$. (You do not
have to use part (b) to solve this part.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
