## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $T$ be a torus with a disk removed and $M$ a torus with a disk
removed and a meridinal disk attached. Consider a finite complex $X$
that is the union of $T$ and $M$ along their boundaries (call the
common circle $C$).
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Using this decomposition of $X$ write down a presentation for
the fundamental group of $X$ using van Kampen's theorem.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute all the homology groups of $X$ using the Mayer-Vietoris
theorem.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
