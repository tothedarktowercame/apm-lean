# Proof Outline: t92J08

Local existence gives an integral curve through every point.  Compactness
should provide a finite subcover of local existence neighborhoods and hence a
single positive time radius.  Mathlib's
`exists_isMIntegralCurve_of_isMIntegralCurveOn` then constructs a global
integral curve through the initial point.  Uniqueness on overlapping open
intervals makes it agree with the supplied curve.

For the counterexample, direct differentiation gives
`(1/(1-t))' = (1/(1-t))²` on `t < 1`.  A hypothetical global extension is
continuous at `1`, while agreement on `Iio 1` forces values tending to
infinity there, yielding a contradiction.
