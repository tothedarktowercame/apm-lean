## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a smooth vector field on a smooth manifold $M$. A
\emph{complete} integral curve for $X$ is one whose domain is the set of
all real numbers.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give hypotheses on $X$ and/or $M$ that guarantee that every integral
curve for $X$ is contained in a complete integral curve.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give an example showing that hypotheses are needed in (a).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
```
