Let $X$ be the vector space of all functions in $C^1([0,1],\R)$ that
vanish at the point $\alpha$ where $\cos(\alpha)=\alpha$. Consider the
norm $\|x\|=\sup_{t\in [0,1]}|x'(t)|$ defined on $X$. Show that if
$y\in X$ is sufficiently close to $0$ then there is a function $x\in
X$ such that $\sin\circ x+x\circ \cos=y$.
