## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Define three measures on the Borel subsets $E\subset [-2,2]$ as
follows: $\mu_1(E)$ is Lebesgue measure; $\mu_2(E)$ is the Lebesgue
measure of $E\cap (0,1)$; $\mu_3(E)=\int_E x^2 dx$. 

For each pair $i\neq j$ write the Lebesgue decomposition of $\mu_i$
with respect to $\mu_j$. That is, determine a function $f_{i,j}(x)$
defined on $[-2,2]$ and a measure $\nu_{i,j}$ defined on the Borel
subsets of $[-2,2]$ by $\mu_i(E)=\int_E f_{i,j}(x)
d\mu_j(x)+\nu_{i,j}(E)$ such that the measures $\mu_j$ and $\nu_{i,j}$
are mutually singular.
```
