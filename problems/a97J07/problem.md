## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Suppose that $f(z)$ is a continuous complex-valued function on the
closed disk $\bar{\Delta}=\{z\in \C \pipe |z|\leq 1\}$ and analytic on
the open disk $\Delta=\{z\in \C \pipe |z|<1\}$. Assume that
$|f(z)|\leq a$ for $|z|=1$ and $\Im(z)>0$, and that $|f(z)|\leq b$ for
$|z|=1$ and $\Im(z)<0$. Prove that $|f(0)|\leq (ab)^{1/2}$. Hint:
consider both $f(-z)$ and $f(z)$.
```
