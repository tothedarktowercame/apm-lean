Assume that $f$ and $g$ are one-to-one analytic functions on the open
disk $\Delta=\{z\in \C\pipe |z|<1\}$. Also assume that $f(0)=g(0)$ and
$f(\Delta)\subset g(\Delta)$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What inequality holds between the numbers $|f'(0)|$ and
$|g'(0)|$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What relationship exists between the two functions in the case
$|f'(0)|=|g'(0)|$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
