## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $S$ be a two-dimensional torus with an open disk removed. Suppose
$\tilde{S}\rightarrow S$ is a regular covering whose group of covering
transformations is cyclic of order $n$ for some integer $n>0$. Show
that $\tilde{S}$ has exactly $n$ boundary components.
```
