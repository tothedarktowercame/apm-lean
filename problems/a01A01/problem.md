## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Find a sequence of real-valued nonnegative functions $\{f_k\}$ on
$[0,1]$ such that $\forall x: \limsup f_k(x)=\infty$ and
$\int_{[0,1]}f_kdx\rightarrow 0$. Adapt this argument to the case
where the domain is $\Rn$.
```
