## Problem Statement

Let $X$ be a Banach space and $B(X)$ the space of all bounded linear
operators on $X$ equiped with the norm $\|T\|=\sup\{\|Tx\|_{X}\pipe
x\in X,\|x\|_{X}=1\}$. Denote by $GL(x)$ the set of all operators
$T\in (X)$ with bounded inverses. Prove that the map $F:T\mapsto
T^{-1}$ is Fr\'echet differentiable on $GL(X)$.
