## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a compact metric space and let $U$ be an open cover of
$X$. Show that $U$ has a Lebesgue number, \ie\ show that there is an
$\epsilon>0$ such that for each subset $C\subset X$ of diameter less
than $\epsilon$, $C$ is contained in some member of $U$.
```
