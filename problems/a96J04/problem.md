Let $f:[0,1]\rightarrow \R$ be an absolutely continuous monotone
increasing function. Prove that if $E\subset [0,1]$ has Lebesgue
measure zero, then $f(E)=\{f(x)\pipe x\in E\}$ also has Lebesgue
measure zero.
