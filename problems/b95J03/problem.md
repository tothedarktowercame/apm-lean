## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $F\subset E$ be a field extension. 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there is a unique $F$-linear transformation 
$\varphi:E \bigotimes_F E\rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_F(E,E)$ such that
$\forall a,b,c\in E:(\varphi(a\otimes b))(c)=abc$ .
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Now (and hereafter) suppose that the extension is finite and 
let $n=[E:F]$ be its degree. What is the dimension of the image of 
$\varphi$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item What is the dimension of the kernel of $\varphi$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give a basis for the kernel of $\varphi$ in the special case
that $F=\R$ and $E=\C$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
