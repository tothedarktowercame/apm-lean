## Problem Statement

Let $Y$ be the solid torus $D^1\times \Son$ in $\C^2$ with boundary
$\Ttw=\Son\times \Son$. Let $f:\Ttw\rightarrow \Ttw$ be the $3$-fold
covering map $f(z,w)=(z,w^3)$. Prove that there is no continuous
function $F:Y\rightarrow \Ttw$ that extends $f$.
