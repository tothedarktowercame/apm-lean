Suppose that $f(z)$ is an entire function such that $\forall z\in
\C:|f(z)|\leq Be^{A|z|}$ for some positive constants $A$ and $B$. Let
$\omega_1,\omega_2,\ldots $ be zeros of $f$ listed with appropriate
multiplicity. Prove that
$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+|\omega_n|)^{-\alpha}<\infty$ for all
$\alpha>1$.
