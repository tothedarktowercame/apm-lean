## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Prove the following special case of the No Retraction theorem. 

Let $B^2$ be the unit ball in $\Rtw$ and let $\partial B^2$ be its
boundary (the unit circle). Show that there is no smooth (or PL, your
choice) map $g:B^2\rightarrow \partial B^2$ such that $g|\partial B^2$
is the identity.
```
