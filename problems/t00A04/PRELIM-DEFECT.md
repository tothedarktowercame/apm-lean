# Prelim defect

As printed, the assertion is false for a zero-dimensional closed oriented
manifold: take `X` to be a one-point manifold, any `Y` containing it, and the
constant retraction.  The inclusion of `X` is itself constant.

The reopened theorem states the closest topological correction: `X` is compact,
Hausdorff, path connected, and infinite.  In the intended smooth setting,
positive dimension implies infinitude.  Smoothness is stronger than the
continuity used by the obstruction.
