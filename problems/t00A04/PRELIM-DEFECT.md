# Prelim defect

As printed, the assertion is false for a zero-dimensional closed oriented
manifold: take `X` to be a one-point manifold, any `Y` containing it, and the
constant retraction.  The inclusion of `X` is itself constant.

The minimal correction is that `X` is nonempty and has positive dimension.
The empty manifold and a one-point zero-manifold are counterexamples, since
their inclusions are constant.  No connectedness or path-connectedness is
needed: a nonempty compact oriented manifold of positive dimension already has
a nonzero top-dimensional fundamental class on at least one component.  The
reopened theorem therefore adds only nonemptiness and positive dimension and
uses C-infinity, rather than analytic, manifold and map hypotheses.
