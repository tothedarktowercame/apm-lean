## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and let $I$ be an ideal of
$R$ with $I\neq R$. Let $b\in R$ and let $\mathrm{Ann}(b)=\{c\in
R\pipe bc=0\}$.

Assume that $\mathrm{Ann}(b)\subset I$.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item There is a natural way of defining a balance map from
$bR\times R/I$ onto $R/I$. Define it, show it is well defined, and
that it is balance.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $bR\otimes R/I\cong R/I$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Now suppose that $b\in I$ and also $\mathrm{Ann}(b)\subset I$.

Consider the exact sequence $0\rightarrow bR\rightarrow R$. Here the
map $bR\rightarrow R$ is simply the inclusion map.

Show that when this exact sequence is tensored with $R/I$ the
resulting sequence is not exact.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
