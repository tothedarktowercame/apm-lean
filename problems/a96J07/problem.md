## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f(z)$ be an entire function. Define $\gamma:[0,1]\rightarrow \C$
by $\gamma(t)=Re^{2\pi it}$.  If $|a|<R$ and $b<R$, evaluate the
integral $\int_{\gamma} f(z)/(z-a)(z-b) dz$ and use the result to give
a proof of Liouville's theorem.
```
