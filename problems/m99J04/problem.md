\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item State the spectral theorem for a self-adjoint compact operator
$T$ defined on a Hilbert space $H$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that the continuous linear operator $A:H\rightarrow H$
is invertible and that $T:H\rightarrow H$ is compact and self-adjoint.
Prove that $A^*TA$ is compact and self-adjoint.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that there is a basis $\{v_{\alpha}\}$ of solutions to
$(T-\lambda P)v_{\alpha}=0$, where $P=(A^*)^{-1}A^{-1}$. Is this basis
orthonormal?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
