## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $F$ be a finite subset of $\C$ and define $D$ to be $\C \setminus
F$.  Let $f$ be an analytic function on $D$ with the properties
$\lim_{|z|\rightarrow \infty}|f(z)|=\infty$ and $\forall z_{\alpha}\in
F:\lim_{|z|\rightarrow z_{\alpha}}|f(z)|=\infty$. Show that $f(D)=\C$.
```
