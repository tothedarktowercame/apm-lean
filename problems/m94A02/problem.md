## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix with real entries. Define $B$ by
$B=(A+A^t)/2$, where $A^t$ is the transpose of $A$. Let the
eigenvalues of $B$ be $\sigma_1\geq \sigma_2\geq \ldots \geq
\sigma_n$. Prove that each eigenvalue $\lambda$ of $A$ satisfies the
inequality $\sigma_1\geq \Re(\lambda)\geq \sigma_n$. What conclusion
can be drawn about the eigenvalues of $A$ in the case $A^t=-A$?
```
