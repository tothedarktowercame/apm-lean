\item On the space $\Rn$, the following norms are defined:
$\|x\|_p=[\sum_{i=1}^n|x_i|^p]^{1/p}$ (for $p\geq 1$) and 
$\|x\|_{\infty}=\max_{1\leq i\leq n}|x_i|$.

Prove that $\lim_{p\rightarrow \infty} \|x\|_p=\|x\|_{\infty}$ for 
each $x$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that the norm in a real Hilbert space is differentiable
at each nonzero point.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
