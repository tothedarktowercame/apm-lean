## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
A metric space $X$ is \emph{totally bounded} if and only if for for
each $\epsilon>0$, $X$ is covered by finitely many
$\epsilon$-balls. Prove or give a counterexample to the following: every
totally bounded complete metric space is compact.
```
