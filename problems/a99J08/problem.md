## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $D=\{z\in \C \pipe |z|<1\}$ and $\Im(z)=y>0$. Write $\bar{D}$ for the
closure of $D$. Assume that $g:\bar{D}\rightarrow \C$ is continuous
and $g$ restricted to $D$ is holomorphic. Further assume that
$\Re(g(x+iy))=0$ for $y=0$ and $x>0$ and that $\Im(g(x+iy))=0$ for $y=0$
and $x<0$. Show that the function $Z\rightarrow |g(z)|/|z|^{1/2}$ is
bounded on $\bar{D}$.
```
