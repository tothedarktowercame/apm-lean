## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $k(x,y)$ be a Lebesgue measurable function from $\Rn\times \Rn$ to
$\R$ such that $\int_{\Rn}|k(x,y)|dx\leq 1$ almost everywhere with
respect to $y$ and $\int_{\Rn}|k(x,y)|dy\leq 1$ almost everywhere with
respect to $x$. Prove that $u\in L^2(\Rn) \Rightarrow \| \int_{\Rn}
k(\cdot ,y) u(y)dy\|_{L^2(\Rn)}\leq \|u\|_{L^2(\Rn)}$.
```
