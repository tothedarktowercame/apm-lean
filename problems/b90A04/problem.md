## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $T$ be a commutative integral domain and let $R$ be a subring of
$T$ with the same multiplicative identity. Let $C=\{x\in R \pipe
\forall t\in T: xt\in R\}$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $C$ is an ideal in $R$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $C$ is an ideal in $T$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $I$ is an ideal of $R$ that is also an ideal of $T$,
prove that $I\subset C$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Now (and for the rest of the problem) 
assume that $P$ is a prime ideal of $R$ and suppose that $x\in
C\setminus P$.  For $t\in T$, show that $xt\in P$ if and only if
$x^2t\in P$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $Q=\{t\in T \pipe xt\in P\}$. Prove that $Q$ is a prime 
ideal of $T$ and that $Q\cap R=P$. Hint: part (d) is useful.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $Q'$ is a prime ideal of $T$ such that $Q'\cap R=P$.
Prove $Q=Q'$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
