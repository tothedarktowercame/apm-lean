Let $\Delta=\{z\in \C \pipe |z|<1\}$. Suppose the series
$\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n$ is absolutely convergent for every $z\in
\Delta$.  If $|z+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n|<1$ for all $z\in \Delta$,
prove that $a_n=0$ for all $n\geq 2$. State clearly any theorems you
use.
