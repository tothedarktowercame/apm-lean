## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Consider the $2$-torus $\Ttw$ to be the smooth manifold obtained as
the quotient group $\Rtw/\Z^2$ and let $\pi:\Rtw\rightarrow \Ttw$ be
the projection map. Let $\omega=\sin^2(2\pi x)\cos^2(2\pi y) dx\wedge
dy$ be a $2$-form on $\Rtw$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that there is a unique $2$-form $\omega'$ on $\Ttw$ with
$\pi^*(\omega')=\omega$. Compute $\int_{\Ttw}\omega'$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find a $1$-form $\eta$ on $\Rtw$ with $d\eta=\omega$. Does there
exist a $1$-form $\eta'$ on $\Ttw$ with $d\eta'=\omega'$?
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute $\int_{\gamma}\eta$ where $\eta$ is the $1$-form you
computed in (b) and $\gamma$ is the closed piecewise linear curve
bounding the parallelogram with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(2,1)$ and
$(1,1)$ (taken in this order
).
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
