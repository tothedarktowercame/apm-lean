## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
In this problem $R$ is a commutative ring with identity $1$. The
following notation and terminology will be used. $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is
the set of prime ideals of $R$. For $R$ to be \emph{$0$-dimensional}
means that if $P,Q\in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ and $P\subset Q$ then $P=Q$.
For $R$ to be \emph{reduced} means that the intersection of all prime
ideals of $R$ is $\{0\}$. Finally, $e\in R$ is \emph{idempotent} if
$e^2=e$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Explain why it is that if $R$ is $0$-dimensional then $R/P$ is
a field for every $P\in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that for every element $a\in R$ there is an idempotent
$e\in R$ such that $aR=eR$. Show that $R$ is $0$-dimensional and
reduced.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $(F_i)_{i\in I}$ be a family of fields. Show that the direct
product ring $\prod_{i\in I}F_i$ is $0$-dimensional and reduced.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $R$ is $0$-dimensional and reduced. For each $P\in
\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ let $F_P=R/P$. Describe a natural ring homomorphism
$\varphi:R\rightarrow \prod_{P\in \mathrm{Spec}(R)}F_P$ and prove that
it is injective.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is finite then the map $\varphi$ 
in part (d) is bijective.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
