## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f(z)$ be analytic on the open unit disk $\Delta$ and have the
power series expansion $f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a(n)z^n$. Assume that
the radius of convergence of this power series is $1$. Also assume
that $f$ extends by analytic continuation to a meromorphic function on
a domain $D$ which contains the closed unit disk $\bar{\Delta}$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $f$ must have at least one pole on the boundary of 
$\bar{\Delta}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If each pole of $f$ which occurs on the boundary of
$\bar{\Delta}$ has order $1$, show that the sequence $a(n)$ is bounded.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
