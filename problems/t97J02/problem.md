## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be the connected sum of four projective planes. Determine
which compact connected $2$-manifolds $5$-fold covering $X$. Repeat
for $6$-fold coverings. Note: a list is not a sufficient answer. You
must show that your manifolds are indeed coverings and that no other
appropriate covers exist.
```
