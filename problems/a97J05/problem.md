## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
If $f(z)$ is analytic in $\{z\in \C \pipe |z|<2\}$ except for a pole
of order $1$ and residue $A$ at $z=1$ and $f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty
}a(n)z^n$ is the power series expansion of $f$ in $\{z\in \C\pipe 
|z|<1\}$, show that $\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}a(n)=-A$.
```
