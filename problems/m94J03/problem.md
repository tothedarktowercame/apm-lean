## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space over the field of complex numbers and let
$A:H\rightarrow H$ be a compact Hermitian operator. Prove that the
resolvent $R_z(A)=(z-A)^{-1}$ satisfies the inequality $\|R_z(A)\|\leq
[\mathrm{dist}(z,\mathrm{spectrum}(A))]^{-1}$ for all $z\in \C$.
```
