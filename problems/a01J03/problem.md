## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $f_n:\R\rightarrow [0,\infty)$ be nondecreasing for each $n\in
\N$. For all $x\in \R$, assume that $f(x)\equiv
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n(x)<\infty$. Prove that
$f'(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n'(x)$ almost everywhere.
```
