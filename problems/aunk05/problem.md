## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Suppose $f_1,f_2,\ldots $ are holomorphic in a region $\Omega$ and
that $f_n$ converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ to a
function $f$. Show
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $f$ is holomorphic in $\Omega$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $f'_n$ converges uniformly to $f'$ on compact subsets of $\Omega$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
