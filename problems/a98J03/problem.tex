Let $f\in L^2(\R)$. Define
$g_y(x)=\frac{y}{\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(x-t)+y^2}f(t)dt$
for $y>0$.

Show that
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $\forall y>0:g_y\in L^2(\R)$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item the map $f\rightarrow g_y$ defines a family of bounded linear
operators from $L^2(\R)$ to $L^2(\R)$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $g_y\rightarrow f$ in $L^2(\R)$ as $y\rightarrow 0$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
