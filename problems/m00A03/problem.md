## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $X$ be a Banach space.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $Y$ is a proper closed subspace of the subspace $Z$
of $X$ (\ie\ $Y\subset Z$ and $Y\neq Z$ and $Y$ is a subspace). Show
that there is a $z\in Z$ such that $\|z\|=1$ and
$\text{dist}\{z,Y\}\geq \frac{1}{2}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $A:X\mapsto X$ be be a compact linear operator. Show that
$0$ is the only possible limit point of the $\sigma(A)$ of $A$. Hint:
consider the set $\sigma(A)\cap \{z \pipe |z|>\epsilon\}$ where
$\epsilon>0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
