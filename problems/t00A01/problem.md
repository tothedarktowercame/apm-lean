## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
By an \emph{integral homology $3$-sphere} we mean a closed,
triangulated $3$-manifold $M$ with $H_k(M;\Z)\cong H_k(\Sth;\Z)$ for
all $k\geq 0$. Let $M_1$ and $M_2$ be integral homology
$3$-spheres. Define $X$ to be the connect sum $M_1\#M_2$ (that is, for
embeddings $\varphi_i:D^3\rightarrow M_i$ for $i=1,2$ of the $3$-disk
$D^3$, form $X$ as the identification space by identifying
$\varphi_1(\partial D^3)$ in $M_1\setminus \varphi_1(D^3)$ with
$\varphi_2(\partial D^3)$ in $M_2\setminus \varphi_2(D^3)$). You may
assume that $\varphi_i(D^3)$ is a $3$-simplex in the triangulation of
$M_i$ ($i=1,2$).
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute the fundamental group of $X$ in terms of those of $M_1$
and $M_2$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Compute all of the homology groups of $X$. (You do \emph{not}
need to compute the homology groups of $\Sth$.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\soln{(Part 2) Though you may need to know what they are in order
to solve the problem.}
```
