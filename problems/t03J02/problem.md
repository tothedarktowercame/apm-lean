## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
Let $T_1$ and $T_2$ be tori and let $J_1$ and $J_2$ be homotopically
trivial simple closed curves on $T_1$ and $T_2$ respectively. Let $X$
be the quotient space obtained by identifying $J_1$ and $J_2$ by a
homeomorphism.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Use Van Kampen's theorem to compute the fundamental group of
$X$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Use the Mayer-Vietoris theorem to compute all homology groups of
$X$
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
```
