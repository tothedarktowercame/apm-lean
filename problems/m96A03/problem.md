## Problem Statement

Raw TeX source:

```tex
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $\{u_1,u_2,\ldots \}$ be an orthonormal basis for
$L^2([a,b])$. Prove that for all $t$ in the interval $[a,b]$,
$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|\int_a^tu_n(s)ds|^2=t-a$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $A$ be a bounded linear operator on a Hilbert space. Assume that
the range of $A$ is closed. Prove that the equation $Ax=b$ has a
solution if and only if $b$ is orthogonal to the kernel of
$A^*$. 

Suggestion: prove first that the kernel of $A^*$ is the orthogonal
complement of the range of $A$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
```
