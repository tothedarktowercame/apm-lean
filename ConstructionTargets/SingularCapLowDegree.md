# Low-degree integral singular cap products

This target isolates the generic Alexander--Whitney cap construction formerly
embedded in the experimental surface-intersection development.

An integral degree-one cocycle is represented by a homomorphism from singular
one-chains to the integers that kills degree-two boundaries. Pullback is
composition with the singular-chain map induced by a continuous map.

The module defines cap operations in degrees 1 to 0, 2 to 1, and 3 to 2.
Generator calculations prove the degree-two and degree-three Leibniz
identities. Consequently, capping a two-cycle produces a one-cycle, and caps
of degree-three boundaries are explicit degree-one boundaries.

The construction restricts to cycle objects and descends categorically to an
additive morphism from H2(X; Z) to H1(X; Z), without choosing cycle
representatives. Chain-, cycle-, and homology-level naturality are proved under
continuous postcomposition and cocycle pullback.

This module makes no claim about manifold fundamental classes, Poincare
duality, geometric intersections, or any particular triangulation.
