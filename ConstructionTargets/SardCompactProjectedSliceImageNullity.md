# Compact projected-slice image nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_compact_projectedSlices` combines compact coordinate-
fiber aggregation with independently indexed lower-dimensional projected
slices.

Given a compact localization and a continuous map into the original target,
an explicit continuous-linear product-coordinate identity reduces image
nullity to residual fiber nullity. Containment of every residual fiber in a
projected slice image, together with Sard induction, dimension drop,
smoothness, a surjective residual projection, and zero projected derivatives,
then proves that the complete localized image has measure zero.
