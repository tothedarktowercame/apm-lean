# Finite-dimensional packing cover bound

`finiteDimensional_card_le_enlarged_halfRadius_power` composes the separated
bounded-ball packing inequality, the exact inner-product-space ball-volume
formula, and the polynomial packing-cardinality theorem. It yields
`card(s) ≤ ofReal ((2 * R + ρ) / ρ) ^ finrank ℝ E` while retaining the
fixture's separation, boundedness, positive-radius, and nonzero finite
unit-ball-volume hypotheses.

The remaining consumer work is one maximal-separated cover extraction and
Taylor target-volume exponent estimate before local rank reduction.
