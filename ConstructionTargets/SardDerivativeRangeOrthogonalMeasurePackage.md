# Derivative-range and orthogonal-residual measure package

`exists_range_orthogonal_borel_addHaarMeasure` bundles the canonical Borel
structures and additive Haar measures on the range of a nonsurjective
continuous real-linear map and on its orthogonal complement. It also supplies
the orthogonal residual's `Nontrivial` certificate.

The package is designed for local installation at a critical derivative before
instantiating compact projected-chart fiber nullity.
