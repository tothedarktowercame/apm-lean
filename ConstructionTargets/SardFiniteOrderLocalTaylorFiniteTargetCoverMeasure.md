# Local Taylor finite target-cover measure

`finiteCover_localTaylor_image_measure_le_card_mul_ballVolume` turns a finite
source-ball cover and Taylor remainder estimates restricted to the portion of
the source locus inside each ball into the corresponding finite target-ball
cover. It also bounds the image volume by the number of source centres times
the common target-ball volume.

The theorem preserves the quantitative conclusion of the global finite-cover
adapter while matching the local estimates supplied after choosing a scale
from compact uniform little-o control. Selecting arbitrarily small coefficients
and scales and constructing the finite source covers remain.
