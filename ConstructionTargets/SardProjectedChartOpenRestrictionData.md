# Projected-chart open-restriction data

`openRestriction_data` records the data retained when a projected open partial
homeomorphism is restricted to an open source neighborhood. Its source becomes
the exact chart-source/neighborhood intersection, while the chart function,
first-coordinate formula, inverse `C¹` regularity, and any sourcewise property
are preserved.

This lets a localized rank-upper bound become global on the chart source used
by the exact-rank compact-nullity theorem.
