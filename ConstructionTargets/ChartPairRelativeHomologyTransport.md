# Chart-pair relative homology transport

This target records the point-set and homological part of transporting the
one-dimensional local model generator through a chart.

A homeomorphism of ambient spaces, together with an exact equivalence between
membership in the source and target distinguished subsets, restricts to a
homeomorphism of those subsets. The existing relative singular map and
five-lemma comparison then prove that the induced relative homology map is an
isomorphism in every degree.

The final declarations transport the nonzero generator of the punctured open
one-disk pair and prove that its image remains nonzero.

For a manifold application, geometry must still provide a chart restriction
whose ambient source is the open one-disk and prove that the chart identifies
the deleted origin with the deleted manifold point. No orientation
compatibility, global fundamental class, localization injectivity, or
Poincare-duality statement is asserted here.
