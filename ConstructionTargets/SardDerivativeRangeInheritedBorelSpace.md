# Derivative-range inherited Borel-space certificates

`range_orthogonal_inherited_borelSpace` proves that a continuous linear map's
range and its orthogonal complement are Borel spaces for the measurable
structures inherited from a finite-dimensional Borel target.

Both coordinate subspaces are closed. Their subtype coercions are therefore
measurable embeddings as well as inducing maps, which identifies each
inherited measurable structure with the corresponding Borel structure.
