Sard induction hypothesis to an entire smooth slice.  At each point, a
surjective projection to a nontrivial coordinate space has zero derivative
after composition with the slice.  The projected-slice derivative bridge
therefore proves that every slice point is critical.

The restricted critical locus consequently equals the whole slice set, so the
induction hypothesis yields zero measure for the complete slice image.  The
rank-chart consumer can instantiate this result on its local inverse-coordinate
