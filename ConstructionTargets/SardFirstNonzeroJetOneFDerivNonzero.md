# Order-one jet derivative nonvanishing

`fderiv_ne_zero_of_mem_firstNonzeroJetLocus_one` converts membership in the
order-one first-nonzero-jet locus into nonvanishing of the ordinary Fréchet
derivative. The proof uses the standard equality between the norm of the first
iterated Fréchet derivative and the norm of `fderiv`.

This provides the nonzero derivative hypothesis required by the projected
kernel dimension-drop theorem during pointwise exact-rank chart assembly.
