# Projected smooth-chart target data

`exists_projected_smoothChart_target_data` constructs the projected product
chart determined by a strict derivative and local `C¹` regularity. The chart's
first coordinate is exactly the derivative-range component of the orthogonal
target-coordinate equivalence.

The same certificate includes `C¹` regularity of the chart and its inverse and
surjectivity of the derivative-range coordinate derivative at every point of
the chart source.
