# Finite-order endpoint small-coefficient nullity

`EndpointSmallCoefficientCoverData` packages the source-side data required at exact Taylor exponent equality: a nonnegative remainder coefficient, a positive cover scale, a finite cover with the standard packing-cardinality bound, local Taylor remainder estimates, and a coefficient expression below the chosen tolerance.

`endpoint_image_volume_eq_zero_of_smallCoefficientCoverData` applies the zero-surplus coefficient estimate at every inverse-natural tolerance and concludes that the endpoint image has zero volume. The remaining analytic task is construction of this data from compact uniform little-o Taylor control.
