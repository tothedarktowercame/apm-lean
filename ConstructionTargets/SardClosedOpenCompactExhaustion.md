# Closed locus in an open chart: compact exhaustion

`exists_compact_cover_inter_of_isClosed_isOpen` gives an explicit ambient
countable compact cover of the intersection of a closed locus with an open
chart in a locally compact, second-countable Hausdorff space.

The proof equips the open subtype with its locally compact instance, exhausts
the induced closed subset there, and transports its compact members through
the subtype inclusion. The resulting union is exactly the ambient intersection.
