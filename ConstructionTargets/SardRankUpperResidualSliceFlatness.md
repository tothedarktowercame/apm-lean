# Rank-upper residual-slice flatness

`ImplicitFunctionData.hasFDerivAt_zero_residual_kernelParameter_of_rankUpper`
specializes inverse-chart residual flatness to a derivative field on a local
rank-at-most locus.  Surjectivity of the projected derivative block and the
full derivative rank bound force the complementary residual derivative to
vanish on the projected kernel.

coordinate kernel, the theorem gives a zero Fréchet derivative for the
residual map restricted to the fixed-coordinate inverse slice.  The remaining
consumer edge constructs these projected data at each localized exact-rank
