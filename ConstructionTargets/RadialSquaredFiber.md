# Squared radial fiber and literal radial level

`RadialSquaredFiber.lean` identifies, at a positive radius, the subtype cut
out by `‖x‖² = r²` with the literal radial level cut out by `‖x‖ = r`.
The equivalence is the identity on the underlying point in the embedded
subset, in both directions.

This is only the representation bridge needed by the corrected `t96J05`
route.  Transport of charted-space and manifold structures, regular-value
existence, and the corrected consumer theorem remain downstream.
