# First-nonzero-jet finite assembly

This module isolates the final finite image-nullity aggregation over the refined jet decomposition.

It splits the finite family indexed by `Icc 1 k` into the order-one first-nonzero locus and the higher loci indexed by `Icc 2 k`. Nullity of those pieces, together with nullity of `flatThrough f K k`, feeds the exact decomposition theorem and yields zero measure for the complete localized image `f '' K`.

The theorem leaves the two analytic inputs explicit: Taylor-flat nullity and nullity of the first-derivative locus. Higher refined loci are supplied by source-dimension induction.
