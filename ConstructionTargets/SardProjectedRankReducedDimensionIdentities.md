# Projected rank-reduced dimension identities

`projected_rank_reduced_finranks` converts an exact derivative-range rank into
the two subtraction identities used by projected-slice induction. The kernel
of the range projection composed with the derivative has source dimension
minus rank, while the orthogonal complement of the derivative range has target
dimension minus rank.

These identities can now be inserted into the finite-order pointwise local
rank-chart packet.
