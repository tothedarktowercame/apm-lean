# Compact projected-chart image nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_compact_projectedChart` proves nullity of a compact
source image inside a product chart.

Agreement between the chart's first coordinate and the first component of a
target-coordinate equivalence represents every target fiber by the matching
fixed-coordinate inverse-chart slice. Lower-dimensional Sard hypotheses for
those slices then feed the compact target-fiber aggregation theorem.
