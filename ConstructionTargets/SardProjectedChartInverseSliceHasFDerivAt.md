# Projected-chart inverse-slice derivative certificate

`exists_hasFDerivAt_inverseSlice_of_contDiffOn_symm` turns continuous differentiability of an open product-chart inverse on its target into a derivative certificate for every fixed-first-coordinate inverse slice.

The all-point slice regularity theorem supplies `ContDiffAt` of order one; differentiability and the canonical `fderiv` certificate then follow.
