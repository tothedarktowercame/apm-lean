# Finite-order closed-open local rank-chart nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_closed_inter_open_finiteOrder_localRankChart` exhausts
a closed locus localized to an open part of a projected rank chart by
countably many compact subsets.  The finite-order compact local rank-chart
theorem discharges each member, and countable null-image aggregation proves
nullity of the full closed-inter-open image.

Pointwise construction of the chart packets and aggregation of their open
cover remain downstream obligations.
