# Target-orthogonal coordinate inverse-slice nullity data

`targetOrthogonalCoordinates_inverseSlice_nullity_data` converts the paired
fiberwise `C¹` and zero-derivative certificates expressed with orthogonal
projection onto a derivative range's complement into the exact second-coordinate
form used by the canonical range/orthogonal target equivalence.

The conversion is an exact equality obtained from
`targetOrthogonalCoordinates_apply`; it introduces no additional analytic
hypotheses.
