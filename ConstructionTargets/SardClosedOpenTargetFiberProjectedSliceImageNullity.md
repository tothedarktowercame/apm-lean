# Closed-in-open target-fiber projected-slice nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_closedOpen_targetFiber_projectedSlices` proves nullity
of a closed locus localized to an open chart from global target-coordinate
fiber containment in lower-dimensional projected slice images.

Continuity and containment restrict to each compact exhaustion member. The
compact target-fiber projected-slice theorem proves memberwise nullity, and the
closed-open aggregation theorem then proves nullity of the complete localized
image.
