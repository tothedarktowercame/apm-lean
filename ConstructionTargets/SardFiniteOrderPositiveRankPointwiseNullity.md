# Finite-order positive-rank pointwise nullity

This theorem combines a localized finite-order projected rank chart with explicitly inherited topology, Borel, measure, and additive-Haar data for the derivative range and its orthogonal residual. Exact positive rank supplies the source and target dimension bounds and the strict projected-kernel dimension drop required by lower-dimensional induction.

For every closed source set, it produces an open neighborhood of the chosen point whose localized image has zero target additive-Haar measure.
