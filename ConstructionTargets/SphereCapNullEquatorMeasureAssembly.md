# Sphere-cap measure assembly with a null equator

`SphereCapNullEquatorMeasureAssembly.lean` combines the canonical measurable
cap partition with ambient equator nullity.  Two applications of
`measure_union` decompose the raw Hausdorff measure of the sphere, the proved
zero equator term is removed, and both strict-cap measures are rewritten by
the reviewed reciprocal-height formulas.



The dimension-two and dimension-three results remain symbolic sums of the two
cap integrals.  They do not use numerical Hausdorff normalization, cancel
extended nonnegative real values, or evaluate sphere masses or moments.
