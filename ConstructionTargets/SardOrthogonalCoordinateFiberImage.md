# Orthogonal-coordinate fiber-image identity

`orthogonalProjection_image_fiber_eq` identifies two representations of a
fixed range-coordinate fiber. Projecting the target-image fiber onto the
orthogonal complement is exactly the same set as mapping those source points
whose range projection is fixed through the residual projection.

This equality transports projected-slice containment into the source-fiber
form required by compact coordinate-fiber nullity.
