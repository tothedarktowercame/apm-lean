# Product nullity from null fibers

`measure_prod_zero_of_forall_fiber_zero` is the Fubini bridge needed to
assemble an uncountable family of projected rank-chart slices.  For a
measurable subset of product coordinates, it upgrades pointwise zero measure
of every vertical section to zero product measure of the whole set.

This avoids any invalid appeal to closure of null sets under uncountable
unions.  The Morse--Sard consumer will identify the vertical sections with the
lower-dimensional inverse-chart slice images and then transfer product nullity
through target coordinates.
