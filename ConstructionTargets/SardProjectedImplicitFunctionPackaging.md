# Projected implicit-function packaging

This module promotes the compiled projected local-rank chart theorem.

The derivative range receives its finite-dimensional completeness instance locally. The range projection and kernel projection, together with their strict derivatives, surjectivity, and complementary kernels, are assembled into `ImplicitFunctionData`. The banked local rank-normal-form theorem then yields an open partial homeomorphism into derivative-range × kernel coordinates whose source contains the base point.

The remaining consumer work is to use the full coordinate formulas to express the exact-rank local upper locus in these coordinates and prove that the residual target coordinate has vanishing derivative along each local rank-at-most fibre before applying Taylor-cover nullity.
