# Point-wedge quotient and saturated open cover

This target constructs the point wedge of two pointed topological spaces as
the quotient of their disjoint union by the equivalence relation generated by
identifying the two basepoints.

`mem_iff_of_eqvGen` proves directly, by induction on `Relation.EqvGen`, that a
subset containing both basepoint representatives is saturated. Consequently,
`preimage_image_of_basepoints_mem` identifies the preimage of its quotient
image exactly. Since the wedge topology is coinduced by `quotientMap`,
`isOpen_image_of_basepoints_mem` derives openness from this exact preimage; it
does not assume that an arbitrary quotient map is open.

The target also supplies `leftPreimage` and `rightPreimage`, their openness,
the quotient open sets `leftOpen` and `rightOpen`, and
`leftOpen_union_rightOpen`. These are the generic open-cover ingredients for
the point-wedge geometry used by frozen `t02A03` and `t97J04`.

The authoritative probe additionally reproduced each frozen problem's local
one-generator relation and built a homeomorphism to that presentation. Those
verification-only declarations are deliberately omitted here: importing a
frozen `Main` from a ConstructionTarget would create a dependency cycle. A
later consumer adapter can prove the corresponding relation equivalence in the
problem-facing direction.
