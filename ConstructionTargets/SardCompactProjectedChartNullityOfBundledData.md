# Compact projected-chart nullity from bundled fiber data

`measure_image_zero_of_bundled_inverseSlice_data` packages the final generic
application of compact projected-chart nullity. Given a compact chart-source
set, a target-coordinate equivalence, the dimension drop, and lower-dimensional
Sard induction, it consumes the paired fiberwise `C¹` and zero-derivative data
as one hypothesis and proves that the compact image has zero target measure.

The residual projection is specialized to the identity continuous linear map.
Thus the bundled orthogonal-residual slice data can be used without another
representation or derivative conversion.
