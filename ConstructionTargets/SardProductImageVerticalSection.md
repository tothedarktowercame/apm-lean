# Product-image vertical sections

`prodMk_preimage_image_eq_snd_image_fstFiber` identifies a vertical section of
a product-valued image exactly.  Fixing the first coordinate in the image is
equivalent to restricting the source to that first-coordinate fiber and taking
the image of the second coordinate.

This equality converts the vertical-section hypothesis of compact product-
image nullity into the fixed-coordinate inverse-chart slice image used by the
lower-dimensional Morse--Sard argument.
