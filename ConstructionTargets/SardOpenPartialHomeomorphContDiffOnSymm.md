# Open partial homeomorphism inverse regularity

`contDiffOn_symm_of_forall_hasFDerivAt_equiv` promotes forward regularity of an open partial homeomorphism to regularity of its inverse across the complete target.

The theorem assumes `ContDiffOn` on the source and an equivalence-valued Frechet derivative at every source point. The pointwise inverse-function regularity theorem then yields `ContDiffOn` for the inverse on the target.
