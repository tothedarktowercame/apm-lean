# Small balls around bounded centers lie in a bounded enlargement

`ball_subset_ball_enlargement_of_center_mem_closedBall` gives the ambient-set
containment needed by the source packing estimate.  If `x` lies in the closed
radius-`R` ball about `z`, then the open radius-`ρ` ball about `x` lies in the
open radius-`R + ρ` ball about `z`.

This metric theorem does not construct a maximal separated family, establish a
polynomial cardinality estimate, or combine that estimate with Taylor target
volumes.  The remaining Sard obligation is one maximal separated-family
construction and finite-dimensional packing/Taylor exponent estimate, before
local rank reduction.
