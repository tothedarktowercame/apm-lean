# Finite-order projected-chart slice certificates

`forall_projectedSlice_image_null_of_lowerDimension` combines retained
finite-order chart regularity, rank-reduced classical Sard admissibility, and
the reviewed zero residual derivatives.  For every fixed first coordinate it
applies lower-dimensional critical nullity to the corresponding residual
inverse-chart slice.

The result is an indexed family of null-image certificates.  Converting this
family into local rank-chart image nullity and aggregating the chart cover are
left to downstream construction targets.
