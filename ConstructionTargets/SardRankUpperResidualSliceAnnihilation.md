# Rank-upper residual slice annihilation

`residual_annihilates_slice_of_rankUpper` converts a zero projected slice
derivative into pointwise residual annihilation.

When the projected ambient derivative is surjective and the full derivative
rank is no larger than the projected target rank, projected-kernel directions
are killed by the full derivative. Consequently every residual coordinate
annihilates all directions supplied by a slice with zero projected derivative.
