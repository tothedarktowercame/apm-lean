# ENNReal ball-volume ratio

`ENNReal.power_volume_ratio` simplifies the quotient of two ball-volume
expressions that share a dimension-dependent constant. Under the explicit
assumptions that the common constant is nonzero and finite, it cancels the
factor and rewrites the quotient of radius powers as `(a / b) ^ d`.

The theorem promotes the compiled fixture without changing its hypotheses or
representation. The remaining consumer work is one polynomial source-cover
radius bound and Taylor target-volume exponent estimate before local rank
reduction.
