# Closed-in-open local image nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_closed_inter_open` aggregates compact member nullity
over the intersection of a closed locus with an open chart in a locally
compact, second-countable Hausdorff source space.

It obtains the ambient compact exhaustion from the closed-open exhaustion
theorem and applies the countable compact image-nullity aggregator. This is the
bridge from compact projected-slice estimates to a complete localized rank
locus.
