# Localized finite-jet induction nullity

`localized_image_zero_of_finiteJet_induction` assembles all three localized
finite-jet branches. Compact Taylor data give arbitrary-additive-Haar nullity
of the flat locus, derivative-range induction handles the order-one locus, and
uniform lower-dimensional induction handles every higher first-nonzero-jet
locus. Their finite union has zero target measure.

The remaining work is a public adapter from the rectangular theorem's stated
regularity and dimension hypotheses to these explicit localized inputs.
