# Projected-chart inverse-slice composition regularity

`contDiffAt_residual_comp_inverseChartSlice` transports pointwise smoothness
through the analytic composition used by projected-chart fibers.

If a fixed-first-coordinate inverse slice is smooth at a slice point and the
ambient map is smooth at the corresponding source point, then composing both
with any continuous-linear residual target coordinate preserves the same
differentiability order.
