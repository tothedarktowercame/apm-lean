# Projected-chart residual-slice nullity data

`residual_inverseSlice_nullity_data` bundles the two fiberwise analytic inputs needed by compact projected-chart nullity: first-order regularity of every residualized inverse slice and a zero Fréchet derivative after any final continuous-linear residual projection.

The regularity follows from two-sided chart and ambient-map regularity, while the zero derivative is transported from the indexed residual-slice certificate through the final projection.
