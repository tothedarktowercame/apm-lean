# Null-image aggregation over critical rank strata

`criticalImage_null_of_rankStrata_null` combines the finite rank decomposition
from `SardCriticalRankStrata` with countable-union nullity.  Its hypothesis says
that the target-measure image of every exact rank stratum indexed by
`r : Fin (finrank ℝ F)` is null; its conclusion is nullity of the image of the
entire nonsurjective locus.

This theorem removes the remaining finite set and measure bookkeeping from the
rectangular Sard program.  It does not prove that a C∞ map sends an exact
derivative-rank stratum to a null set.  That uniform analytic theorem remains
the next obligation, before any manifold chart assembly.
