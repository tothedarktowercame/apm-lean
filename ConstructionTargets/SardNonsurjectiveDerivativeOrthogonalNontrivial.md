# Nonsurjective derivative orthogonal residual nontriviality

`nontrivial_range_orthogonal_of_not_surjective` proves that a nonsurjective
continuous real-linear map into a finite-dimensional real inner-product space
has a nontrivial orthogonal complement to its range.

This converts the critical-locus nonsurjectivity hypothesis directly into the
residual-coordinate `Nontrivial` instance required by compact projected-fiber
nullity.
