# Target orthogonal coordinate equivalence

`targetOrthogonalCoordinates` is the continuous real-linear equivalence from a
finite-dimensional inner-product space to the product of a chosen submodule
and its orthogonal complement.

`targetOrthogonalCoordinates_apply` identifies its two coordinates exactly as
the orthogonal projections onto those subspaces.  Taking the chosen submodule
to be the derivative range gives the range/residual target coordinates used in
the projected Sard rank charts.
