# Equivalence image-nullity transfer

`measure_zero_of_equiv_image_zero` transfers nullity of a set's image under an
equivalence back to the original measure space when the equivalence is
quasi-measure-preserving in that direction.

The proof takes the preimage of the null coordinate image and uses injectivity
to identify that preimage with the original set.  In the Sard rank-chart path,
this isolates the final measure-theoretic transport from the separate task of
proving that the range/complement target-coordinate equivalence is
quasi-measure-preserving.
