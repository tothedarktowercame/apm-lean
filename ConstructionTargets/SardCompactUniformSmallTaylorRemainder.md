# Uniform small Taylor remainders on compact flat loci

`exists_uniform_small_taylor_remainder_on_compact_flat` assumes global C^(k+1)
regularity and vanishing derivatives of orders 1 through k+1 on a compact
locus. Every positive coefficient then admits one positive radius giving the
order-(k+1) remainder bound at every locus base point and every sufficiently
close ambient point.

The proof bounds the highest derivative uniformly near the compact locus,
applies the affine-line derivative and Taylor estimates on convex source balls,
and cancels the factorial by its choice of derivative tolerance.

The endpoint application must still choose the coefficient to meet its volume
tolerance, invoke compact cover construction and nullity, and specialize the
finite-order induction and compact exhaustion.
