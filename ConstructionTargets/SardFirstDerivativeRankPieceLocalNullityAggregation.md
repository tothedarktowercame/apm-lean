# First-derivative rank-piece local nullity aggregation

`rankPiece_image_null_of_locally_image_null` specializes second-countable local
nullity aggregation to one exact derivative-rank piece of the order-one
first-nonzero-jet locus.  It retains the precise set representation used by the
finite rank-stratum assembly.

For every point of the piece, an open neighborhood containing that point and a
null image of the piece restricted to the neighborhood are sufficient.  The
theorem extracts a countable subcover and concludes that the image of the whole
rank piece has measure zero.
