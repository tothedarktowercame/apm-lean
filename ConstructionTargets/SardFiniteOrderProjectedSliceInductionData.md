# Finite-order projected-slice induction data

`finiteOrder_projectedSlice_induction_data` packages the two facts needed to
apply lower-dimensional Sard induction after a rank split.  The residual map
on every inverse-chart slice retains the original finite differentiability
order, and classical Sard admissibility remains valid after subtracting the
same derivative rank from source and target dimensions.

The theorem deliberately leaves the analytic nullity invocation and its local
aggregation to the next construction target.
