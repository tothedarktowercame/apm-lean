# Exact-rank compact projected-chart aggregation

`rankPiece_image_null_of_compact_projectedChart_certificates` aggregates compact image-nullity certificates from every derivative-range chart-source refinement over the complete exact derivative-rank piece.

Its remaining input is deliberately pointwise: each compact subset of the localized rank-upper locus inside the chart refinement must have null image. This is the interface supplied by the compact projected-chart residual-slice theorem.
