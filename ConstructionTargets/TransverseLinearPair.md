# TransverseLinearPair

`IsTransversePair f g` records that the ranges of two linear maps jointly span
their target.  `isTransversePair_iff_surjective_coprod` converts this geometric
form into surjectivity of the tangent-sum map on the product source.

The `t00J02` bridge consumes this interface pointwise for the derivatives of
the surface map and embedded circle.  Its remaining geometric obligation can
therefore be stated using the operational tangent-sum map expected by an
intersection or Poincare-duality construction.
