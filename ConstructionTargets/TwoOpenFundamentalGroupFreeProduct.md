# Two-open fundamental-group free-product principle

`twoOpenFundamentalGroupFreeProduct` is the exact generic proposition isolated
by the `t00A01` consumer probe.  For a space covered by two path-connected open
sets with simply connected overlap, it asks for a nonempty multiplicative
equivalence from the ambient fundamental group at a chosen basepoint to the
free product of the fundamental groups of the two pieces at independently
chosen basepoints.

The declaration is an interface proposition, not a proof of the proposition.
The existing two-open development proves the target-natural fundamental-
groupoid pseudopushout mapping property.  Constructing this proposition from
that result still requires the generic connected-groupoid/connector reduction,
the simply-connected overlap reduction, and the final chosen-basepoint
identification.

For `t00A01`, after an inhabitant of this proposition is supplied, the residual
is the bounded homotopy-equivalence/basepoint transport and factorwise
coproduct adapter.  `t97J04` is the other named consumer.  This target contains
no Lefschetz argument, covering-space argument, or specialization to either
consumer.
