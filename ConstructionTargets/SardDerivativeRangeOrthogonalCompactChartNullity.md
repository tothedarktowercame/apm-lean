# Derivative-range orthogonal compact-chart nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_rangeOrthogonal_compactChart` specializes compact
projected-chart nullity to the range of a nonsurjective derivative and its
orthogonal complement. It locally installs proper, locally compact,
second-countable, inherited-Borel, nontrivial, and additive-Haar structures,
then converts orthogonal-projection slice data to canonical target-coordinate
data and proves that the compact source image has zero target measure.

The remaining consumer work is geometric: provide the concrete exact-rank
chart, localized rank and slice data, dimension drop, and lower-dimensional
induction hypothesis.
