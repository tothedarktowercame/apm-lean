# Compact-image measurability

`measurableSet_image_of_isCompact_continuousOn` supplies the measurability
hypothesis required by product-measure null-fiber aggregation.  A map
continuous on a compact localization has compact image; in a Hausdorff Borel
target that image is closed and therefore measurable.

The Morse--Sard consumer uses this bridge after compactly localizing projected
rank-chart coordinate images, before applying Fubini to their vertical slices.
