# Projected-slice fiber image nullity

`forall_fiber_image_null_of_subset_projectedSlices` packages the analytic
fiber obligation used by compact product-coordinate aggregation.  For each
fixed first coordinate, the residual image is assumed to lie in the image of a
lower-dimensional slice.

The lower-dimensional Sard induction theorem makes every containing slice
image null from smoothness, dimension drop, a surjective residual projection,
and vanishing projected derivative.  Measure monotonicity then gives nullity
of every fixed-coordinate residual fiber image.
