# Compact coordinate-fiber image nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_compact_coordinate_fibers` packages the compact Fubini
step in original target coordinates.  After a continuous-linear equivalence to
product coordinates, it assumes that the second-coordinate image of every
fixed-first-coordinate source fiber is null.

The coordinate image is measurable because the source localization is compact
and the map is continuous.  Fiberwise nullity makes that product image null,
and Haar nullity is then reflected through the continuous-linear equivalence
to the original target image.
