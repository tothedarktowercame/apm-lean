# Projected-coordinate explicit inherited Borel data

`projectedCoordinates_explicit_inherited_borelData` states the Borel-space
proofs for the derivative range and orthogonal residual with both implicit
parameters made explicit. The topology and measurable-space arguments are
therefore fixed to the instances inherited by the subtypes.

This orientation prevents the positive-rank pointwise nullity adapter from
introducing fresh coordinate measurable-space metavariables when it installs
Borel and additive-Haar data.
