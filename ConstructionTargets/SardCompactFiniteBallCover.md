# Finite ball covers of compact source patches

`IsCompact.exists_finset_ball_cover` packages compactness in the form needed by
the Sard scale argument.  A compact subset of a pseudometric space is contained
in the union of finitely many open balls of any prescribed positive radius.

This theorem provides existence of a finite cover, but no quantitative bound on
the number of centers.  It also does not derive Taylor image estimates or
target-ball measure bounds.  The remaining Sard obligation is one quantitative
smooth flat-locus Taylor/exponent estimate over finite-dimensional source
covers, before local rank reduction.
