# Exact-rank closed-open aggregation

`rankPiece_image_zero_of_pointwise_closedOpen` chooses the null-image
neighborhood supplied at every point of an order-one exact-rank critical
piece. Inclusion in the closed rank-upper locus transfers each local nullity
certificate, and second countability aggregates them over the complete piece.

The remaining work is finite aggregation across the possible critical ranks.
