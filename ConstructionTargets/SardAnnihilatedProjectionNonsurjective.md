# Annihilated-projection nonsurjectivity

`LinearMap.not_surjective_of_surjective_comp_eq_zero` proves that a linear map
cannot be surjective when a surjective projection to a nontrivial module
annihilates its entire range.  Otherwise the zero composite would itself be
surjective, contradicting nontriviality of the projected codomain.

The Morse--Sard consumer applies this bridge to the derivative of a projected
inverse-chart slice.  Its constant projected coordinate makes the composite
derivative zero, so the sliced map lies in the critical locus consumed by the
compiled lower-dimensional image-nullity induction.
