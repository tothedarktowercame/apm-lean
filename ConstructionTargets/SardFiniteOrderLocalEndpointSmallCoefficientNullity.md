# Local endpoint small-coefficient nullity

`LocalEndpointSmallCoefficientCoverData` records, for one target tolerance, a
nonnegative Taylor coefficient, a positive source scale, a finite source cover
with its packing-cardinality bound, localized remainder estimates on each
covering ball, and the required strict coefficient bound.

`endpoint_image_volume_eq_zero_of_localSmallCoefficientCoverData` applies the
localized zero-surplus coefficient estimate at every inverse-natural tolerance
and concludes that the endpoint image has zero volume. Constructing the data
from compact uniform little-o Taylor control and maximal separated covers is
the remaining analytic obligation.
