# Flat-through Taylor nullity

This module applies the quantitative multivariable Taylor-covering theorem to the exact `flatThrough` locus used by the refined jet decomposition.

Membership in `flatThrough f K k` supplies vanishing of every iterated Fréchet derivative from order one through `k`. Given compactness, a containing closed ball, positive exponent surplus, sufficient smoothness, and the finite-dimensional volume constants, the Taylor theorem therefore yields zero volume for the image of the flat locus.

The rectangular assembly must still derive the locus's compactness and boundedness from its localization and handle the separate first-derivative rank locus.
