# Smooth projected-chart first-derivative surjectivity

`range_eq_top_of_eq_fst_smooth_chart` proves that the derivative of a function equal to the first coordinate of a two-sided smooth open product chart is surjective at every chart-source point.

It obtains the forward and inverse derivatives from `ContDiffOn`, differentiates the local inverse identity, and identifies the given derivative with the chart derivative's first block.
