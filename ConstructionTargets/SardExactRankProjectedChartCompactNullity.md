# Exact-rank projected-chart compact nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_exactRank_projectedChart` packages the final compact
nullity step for a concrete exact-rank projected chart.  Its rank bound and
projected-surjectivity hypotheses produce zero derivatives on all orthogonal
residual inverse slices; the existing bundle and coordinate theorems then
apply compact projected-chart nullity in the original target Haar measure.

The remaining consumer work is pointwise construction of these chart
hypotheses, compact localization, and aggregation of the exact-rank pieces.
