# First-nonzero-jet inductive locus nullity

This module aggregates the scalarized implicit-chart witness available at every point of a higher first-nonzero-jet locus.

For each point, the pointwise instantiation theorem supplies an open neighborhood whose restricted locus image is null under the lower-dimensional local Sard hypothesis. Second-countability then reduces the open family to the compiled countable local-nullity aggregation, proving that the image of the entire refined locus has zero measure.

The remaining assembly combines these higher refined loci with Taylor-flat nullity and handles the separate first-derivative rank case.
