# Closed-in-open projected-chart image nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_closedOpen_projectedChart` proves nullity for a closed
locus localized inside an open projected-chart source. Its fiber domains retain
only coordinates whose inverse-chart images lie in that locus, so slice
regularity and zero-derivative hypotheses are needed precisely where the
closed-locus data apply.

This supports derivative rank-upper loci without incorrectly requiring those
closed sets to be open chart sources.
