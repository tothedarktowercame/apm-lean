# All-point projected-chart inverse-slice regularity

`contDiffAt_inverseSlice_of_contDiffOn_symm` turns one smoothness certificate
for a product-chart inverse on its open target into pointwise smoothness of
every fixed-first-coordinate inverse slice.

Openness upgrades within-target smoothness to ordinary pointwise smoothness,
and composition with the smooth insertion `w ↦ (r, w)` gives the slice result.
