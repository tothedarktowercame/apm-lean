# Local endpoint Taylor coefficient bound

`endpoint_localTaylor_image_measure_le_coefficient` combines a finite source
cover, its packing-cardinality estimate, and Taylor remainder bounds localized
to the individual covering balls. At exact exponent equality, the source scale
cancels and the image-volume bound is proportional to the Taylor coefficient
raised to the target dimension.

This is the localized counterpart of the zero-surplus endpoint estimate. The
remaining task is to choose arbitrarily small coefficients and corresponding
cover scales from compact uniform little-o Taylor control and package the
resulting cover data.
