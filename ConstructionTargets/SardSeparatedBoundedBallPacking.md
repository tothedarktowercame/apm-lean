# Separated bounded-ball packing inequality

`finset_card_mul_le_measure_ball_of_separated` combines the reviewed
half-radius disjointness, bounded-enlargement containment, and finite packing
measure lemmas. A finite radius-`ρ` separated family inside `closedBall z R`,
whose half-radius balls each have measure at least `v`, satisfies
`card(s) * v ≤ μ (ball z (R + ρ / 2))`.

containment hypotheses. The remaining consumer work is one maximal-separated
