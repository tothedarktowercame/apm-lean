# Compact projected-chart nullity from residual-slice data

`measure_image_zero_of_residualSlice_data` turns the bundled analytic data on every fixed-first-coordinate inverse slice into nullity of a compact chart-source image.

It identifies the residual slice with the second target coordinate, obtains its first-order regularity and projected zero derivative from the bundled theorem, and applies the banked compact projected-chart nullity result.
