# Continuous linear equivalence image nullity

`ContinuousLinearEquiv.measure_zero_of_image_zero_addHaar` reflects nullity
of a coordinate image through a continuous real-linear equivalence between
Borel normed spaces carrying additive Haar measures.

The result specializes the continuous-additive-equivalence Haar comparison to
the linear coordinate maps used by Sard rank charts, then applies the generic
equivalence image-nullity transfer.  The target is assumed locally compact and
second countable, as supplied by the finite-dimensional coordinate spaces.
