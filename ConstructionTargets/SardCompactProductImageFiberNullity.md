# Compact product-image fiber nullity

`measure_prod_image_zero_of_fstFiber_snd_image_zero` presents compact product-
image Fubini aggregation in the source-fiber form used by projected rank
charts.  For every fixed first coordinate, it assumes that the second-
coordinate image of the corresponding source fiber is null.

The exact vertical-section identity converts these hypotheses to null sections
of the product image, and compact product-image nullity then proves that the
whole coordinate image has zero product measure.
