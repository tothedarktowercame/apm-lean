# Derivative-range orthogonal closed-in-open chart nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_rangeOrthogonal_closedOpenChart` specializes the
closed-in-open projected-chart theorem to a derivative range and its orthogonal
complement. It installs properness, second countability, inherited Borel
structures, nontriviality, and additive Haar measures locally, then uses the
canonical target-coordinate equivalence.

The remaining work is concrete pointwise smooth-chart and localized residual
slice instantiation followed by exact-rank aggregation.
