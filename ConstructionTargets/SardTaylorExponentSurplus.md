# Taylor exponent surplus selection

`exists_taylor_exponent_surplus` chooses a finite Taylor cutoff and a strictly
positive covering surplus from source and target dimensions alone. Positivity
of the target finrank follows from target nontriviality, and choosing the
cutoff equal to the source finrank makes the product strictly exceed the source
dimension.

This removes the cutoff and surplus witnesses from the eventual rectangular
Morse--Sard interface.
