# Finite-order positive-rank pointwise data

`exists_positiveRank_dimension_inheritedMeasure_data` combines exact positive
derivative rank and nonsurjectivity into the dimension and measure data needed
by the finite-order pointwise nullity argument.

The result contains both ambient rank bounds, strict dimension drop for the
projected kernel, inherited Borel structures on the range coordinates,
nontriviality of the orthogonal residual, and compatible additive-Haar
measures. The final pointwise adapter can destructure this packet while keeping
the coordinate measurable spaces fixed.
