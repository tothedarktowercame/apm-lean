# Projected-slice derivative nonsurjectivity

`not_surjective_fderiv_of_projected_hasFDerivAt_zero` is the calculus-facing
form of the annihilated-projection bridge.  The chain rule identifies the
derivative of a projected slice with the projection composed with the slice
derivative.  Uniqueness against the supplied zero derivative shows that this
linear composite vanishes.

When the projection is surjective and its codomain is nontrivial, the compiled
linear bridge then proves that the original slice derivative is not
surjective.  This supplies the critical-locus hypothesis required by the
lower-dimensional Morse--Sard induction.
