# Finite-order one-dimensional endpoint cover nullity

`OneDimEndpointBallCover` records a finite family of target balls covering the image in a one-dimensional target whose total volume is below a prescribed tolerance.

`volume_image_eq_zero_of_oneDim_endpointBallCovers` combines such certificates at every positive tolerance with finite-cover measure aggregation and the inverse-natural limit theorem. Thus the remaining analytic task is precisely to construct these certificates from uniform little-o Taylor control at the equality endpoint.
