# Finite-order projected smooth-chart target data

`exists_projected_finiteOrderChart_target_data` constructs the projected local
rank chart at an arbitrary nonzero finite differentiability order. It retains
the target range-coordinate formula, regularity of the chart and its inverse at
that order, and pointwise surjectivity of the range-coordinate derivative.

The remaining step localizes this chart packet to the closed rank-upper locus
and supplies the indexed residual-slice zero data used by pointwise nullity.
