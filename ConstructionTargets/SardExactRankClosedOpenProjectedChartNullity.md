# Exact-rank closed-in-open projected-chart nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_exactRank_closedOpen_projectedChart` combines a
smooth projected chart, its canonical derivative-range coordinate, and a rank
bound restricted to a closed locus inside an open chart neighborhood. The
locus-restricted residual-slice theorem supplies the zero derivatives required
by derivative-range closed-in-open nullity.

The remaining work is pointwise projected-chart construction followed by
aggregation of the exact-rank pieces.
