# Finite-order rank-locus local aggregation

`measure_image_zero_of_rankLocus_pointwise_open` packages the
second-countable aggregation step for rank loci.  From an existential null
open neighborhood at every point, it chooses an indexed neighborhood family
and invokes the reviewed countable local-image nullity theorem.

Constructing the finite-order projected chart witness at each point and
assembling the finitely many derivative ranks remain downstream obligations.
