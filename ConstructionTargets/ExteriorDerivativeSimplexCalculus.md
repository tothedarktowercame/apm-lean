# Exterior derivative and simplex calculus

This leaf contains the local computations used by `t98A05`: the displayed
coordinate formulas for the exterior derivatives of the one-, two-, and
three-forms, plus the integral of the resulting constant value `2` over the
standard oriented parameter triangle. The triangle has area `1/2`, so the
integral is `1`.

The exterior-derivative lemmas take explicit differentiability hypotheses;
they do not infer regularity from an unbundled pointwise specification. The
frozen concrete polynomial forms satisfy those hypotheses independently.

No circle-period or global non-differential assertion is made or assumed.
