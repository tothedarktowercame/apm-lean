# Finite-order rank-zero same-space endpoint nullity

For a differentiable self-map of a nontrivial finite-dimensional real normed space, exact derivative rank zero forces the derivative to be the zero linear map and hence to have zero determinant. Mathlib's fixed-dimensional determinant Sard lemma then gives zero additive-Haar measure for the image of the entire rank-zero locus.

This banks the analytic first-order endpoint in a common coordinate space. The remaining endpoint work is transport between equal-dimensional source and target coordinate spaces and the separately classified one-dimensional-target case.
