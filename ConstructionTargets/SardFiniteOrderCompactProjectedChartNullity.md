# Finite-order compact projected-chart nullity

`measure_image_zero_of_compact_projectedChart_of_sliceCertificates` promotes
an indexed family of residual inverse-slice nullity certificates to nullity of
the image of a compact set contained in a projected chart source.  It uses the
reviewed target-fiber containment, compact coordinate-fiber product nullity,
and continuous-linear target-coordinate transfer.

Instantiating the slice certificates from finite-order local rank data and
aggregating the compact chart cover remain downstream obligations.
