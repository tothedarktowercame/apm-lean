# Nonzero-jet scalar separation

This module promotes scalar separation for selected nonzero jet values.

In a real inner-product target, a nonzero vector `w` is separated from zero by
the continuous linear functional `innerSL ℝ w`, whose value at `w` is its
nonzero self-inner-product. The second theorem specializes this construction
directly to a selected nonzero value of `iteratedFDeriv ℝ i f x`.

The result preserves the exact iterated-derivative representation and does not
assert a derivative formula for the fixed-direction scalar component. That
identity and the subsequent implicit-function hypersurface construction remain
the next source-dimension-reduction step.
