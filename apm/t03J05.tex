Let $F$ be a closed smooth oriented surface of genus $2$. 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define two embeddings $\Ttw\rightarrow F\times F$ whose images
have nonzero intersection number. Prove that you are correct.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For some $x\in F$, let $F^*=F\setminus \{x\}$. Give three
embeddings $\Ttw\rightarrow F^*\times F^*$, no two of which are
homotopic in any four manifold $X$ containing $F^*\times F^*$ as
an open subset. Prove that you are correct.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
