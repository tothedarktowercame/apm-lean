Consider the four statements concerning commutative integral domains
$R$.  
\begin{Rlist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $R$ is a principal ideal domain.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $R$ is a unique factorization domain.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $R$ is Noetherian.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $R$ is integrally closed in its field of quotients. (\Ie\ every
element of the field of quotients that is a zero of a monic polynomial
with coefficients in $R$ is in $R$.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{Rlist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that I $\Rightarrow $ II $\Rightarrow $ IV.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give counterexamples with explanations to show that
II $\nRightarrow $ I, II $\nRightarrow $ III, and
III $\nRightarrow $ I. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Give (no explanation needed) an example of an integral domain
that is not a unique factorization domain.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
