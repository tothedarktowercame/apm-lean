On $\C\cong \Rtw$, consider the $1$-form $\alpha=(xdy-ydx)/2\pi$. Let
$\hat{\alpha}$ be the restriction of this form to the unit circle
$\Son$. Let $\varphi:\C\setminus \{0\}\rightarrow \Son$ be the
projection $z/|z|$. Let $\beta=\varphi^*\hat{\alpha}$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Find an explicit formula for $\beta$ and show that $\beta$ is
closed.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Let $f:\Son\rightarrow \C\setminus \{0\}$ be any smooth map.
Recall that the winding number $W(f,0)$ of $f$ around the origin is
defined to be the degree of $u:\Son\rightarrow \Son$ where
$u(z)=f(z)/|f(z)|$. Show that $W(f,0)=\int_{\Son}f^*\beta$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
