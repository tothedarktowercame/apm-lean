Let $x,y,z$ be standard coordinates in $\mathbf{A}^3$ and define the
2-form $\omega=(x-1)^3 dy \wedge dz+ (y-2)^3 dz \wedge dx + (z-3)dx
\wedge dy$. Find the sphere $S$ of unit radius which minimizes
$\int_{S}\omega$ and compute the integral, orienting the sphere as the
boundary of the ball it bounds.
