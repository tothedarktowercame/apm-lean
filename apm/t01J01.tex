Let $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_2$ be two disjoint simple closed curves on
a closed orientable surfaces $S$ of genus $2$ such that $\gamma_i$
($i=1,2$) is homologous to $0$ but not nulhomotopic in $S$. Show there
is a surface $A\subset S$ with $A\cong \Son\times I$ such that
$\partial A=\gamma_1\cup \gamma_2$.
