Let $H^1(\Omega)$ denote the usual Sobolev space over $\Omega$ and let
$H_0^1(\Omega)$ be the closure of $D(\Omega)$ in $H^1(\Omega)$. Find a
basis for the subspace $H_0^1(\Omega)$ in each of the three cases:
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $\Omega=(0,1)$, the unit interval in $\R$;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $\Omega=(0,\infty)$, the half-line;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item  $\Omega=\R$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
