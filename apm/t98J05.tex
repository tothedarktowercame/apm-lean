Let $M$ be an orientable curve of genus $2$ in $\Rth$ centered on the
$z$-axis, with the two holes vertical. Let $T:M\rightarrow M$ be
rotation by $\pi$ about the $z$-axis. Define an equivalence relation
on $M$ where $x\sim y$ if and only if $y=T(x)$. Let $N$ be the
quotient space of $M$ under this equivalence relation and let $p:M
\rightarrow N$ be the quotient map. Then $N$ is a $2$-torus.  Let
$\pi:\Rtw\rightarrow N$ be the universal covering space with covering
translations $(x,y)\rightarrow (x+m,y+n)$. Let $\hat{\alpha}=5dx+dy$
be a $1$-form on $\Rtw$. Let $\alpha$ be a $1$-form on $N$ such that
$\pi^*(\alpha)=\hat{\alpha}$. Then $\beta=p^*(\alpha)$ is a $1$-form
on $M$.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $\beta$ is closed but not exact. (It may be helpful
to work first with $\alpha$ then note that $p$ is a covering map off
of a collection of points.)
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Draw a curve $c$ in $M$ which is homotopically non-trivial but
for which $\int_{c}\beta=0$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
