Let $T$ be a smooth torus (dimension $2$, genus $1$), centered on the
origin. Let $f:T\rightarrow T$ be the map that rotates $T$ by $180$
degrees about the the $x$-axis.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item How many fixed points does $f$ have? Which of these are
Lefschetz fixed points? For each fixed point, compute the local
Lefschetz number.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Is $f$ homotopic to the identity map? Either construct such a
homotopy or show that none exists.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
