Suppose that $\{f_n\}$ is a sequence of analytic functions on a region
$\Omega$ and $F$ is a continuous function on $\Omega$ such that for
any disk $D\subset \Omega$, $\lim_{n\rightarrow
\infty}\int_D|f_n(z)-F(z)|dxdy=0$. Show that $F$ is analytic on
$\Omega$ and that $f_n\rightarrow F$ and $f_n'\rightarrow F'$
uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$.
