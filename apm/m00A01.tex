Let $H$ be a Hilbert space with closed subspaces $M$ and $N$ such that
$M\cap N\{0\}$ and $H=M+N$. Let $P_N:H\rightarrow N$ denote the
orthogonal projection operator. 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define what we mean by the space $N^{\perp}$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define the operator $Q:M\rightarrow N^{\perp}$ by
$Q=I-P_N$. Show that $Q\equiv P_{N^{\perp}}\equiv P_N^{\perp}$ when
the later two operators are restricted to $M$.  Prove that $Q$ is both
injective and surjective.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Prove that there is an $\alpha>0$ such that $\forall x\in
M:\|x-P_Nx\|\geq \alpha\|x\|$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
