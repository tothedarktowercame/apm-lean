Let $g(x,y)=2$ for $0\leq y\leq x\leq 1$ and $g(x,y)=0$ otherwise. Set
$d\mu(x,y)=g(x,y)dxdy$ as a positive finite measure on $\Rtw$. Let $P$
be the projection map from $\Rtw$ to the first coordinate, \ie\
$P(x,y)=x$. Define a new measure $\tau$ on $\R$ by the composition
$\tau=\mu\circ P^{-1}$. Find the Lebesgue decomposition and the
Radon-Nikodym derivative of Lebesgue measure $m$ on $\R$ with respect
to $\tau$.
