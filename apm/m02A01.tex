Let $X$ be a normed linear space.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Define weak convergence in $X$. Discuss and justify uniqueness
properties of weak convergence in $X$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that strong convergence in $X$ implies weak convergence in
$X$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show, with a counterexample, that weak convergence does not imply
strong convergence. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that if $X$ is finite dimensional, then strong convergence
is equivalent to weak convergence.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
