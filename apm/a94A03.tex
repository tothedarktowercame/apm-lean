For $f\in L^p(\R)$ with $1\leq p<\infty$ and $t>0$ define a function
$K_t(f)$ on $\R$ by $K_t(f)(x)
=\frac{1}{t}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-\pi(x-y)^2/t^2}f(y)dy.$ Show
that
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item the operator $K_t$ maps the unit ball of $L^p(\R)$ into itself;
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $K_tf\rightarrow f$ in $L^p$ norm as $t\rightarrow 0$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
