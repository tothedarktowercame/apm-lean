Consider functions $f:\C\rightarrow \C$ as functions from $\Rtw$ to
$\Rtw$ in the usual way. Show that if $f$ is complex differentiable
then (the matrix of) its derivative as a function from $\Rtw$ to
$\Rtw$ has symmetry properties not satisfied by the functions
$g(x,y)\equiv (x,-y)$ and $h(x,y)\equiv (x,2y)$.
