Let $f:\Son\rightarrow \RP^2$ be an embedding. Let $X$ be the space
obtained by attaching a $2$-disk to $\RP^2$ via $f$. Find the
fundamental group and homology groups of $X$ if 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $f$ is homotopically trivial (\ie\ homotopic to the constant map);
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item $f$ is homotopically nontrival.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
