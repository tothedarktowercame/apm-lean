A function $f$ is analytic in a neighborhood of the closure
$\bar{\Delta}$ of $\Delta=\{z\pipe |z|<1\}$ and is one-to-one on
$\bar{\Delta}$. Thus $D=f(\Delta)$ is a domain bounded by a smooth
Jordan curve $C=f(\partial\Delta)$. Given that $f(0)=0$ and
$|f'(0)|=1$, verify that the length $\ell(C)$ of $C$ satisfies
$\ell(C)\geq 2\pi$ and that $\ell(C)>2\pi$ can hold only if $\Delta$
is not contained in $D$.
