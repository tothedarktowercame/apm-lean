Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space over the rational field
$\Q$. Suppose $A\subset V$ is a finitely generated subgroup under
addition.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Show that $A$ is generated (as a group) by some subset of a basis
of $V$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose $G\subset GL(V)$ is a finite subgroup of the group of
invertible linear endomorphisms of $V$ over $\Q$. Suppose $B\subset V$
is a finitely generated abelian group. Let $B'$ be the subgroup
generated by all $g(B)$ for all $g\in G$. You may assume these (easy)
facts: $B'$ is finitely generated; $B\subset B'\subset V$ and
$g(B')\subset B'$ for all $g\in G$. 

Let $\Z$ be the ring of integers and let $R$ be a commutative
ring. The group of $n\times n$ matrices over $R$ with inverses that
are also matrices over $R$ is denoted $GL_n(R)$.

Suppose $GL_n(\Q)$ is the group of invertible $n\times n$ matrices and
$G\subset GL_n(\Q)$ is a finite subgroup. Show that there is a $P\in
GL_n(\Q)$ such that $PGP^{-1}=\{PgP^{-1}\pipe g\in G\}\subset
GL_n(\Z)$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item If $f:R\rightarrow S$ is a ring homomorphism, there is a natural
group homomorphism $GL_n(f):GL_n(R)\rightarrow GL_n(S)$ defined by
letting $f$ act on each entry. 

Let $m\geq 2$. Show that the kernel of the natural map
$GL_n(\Z/p^m\Z)\rightarrow GL_n(\Z/p^{m-1}\Z)$ is abelian. Hint: this
kernel consists of elements of the form $1+p^{m-1}A$ for $A\in
M_n(\Z/p^m\Z)$, the ring of matrices over $\Z/p^m\Z$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item Suppose that $G\subset GL_n(\Q)$ as above is a finite simple group.
Show that there is an injection $G\rightarrow GL_n(\Z/p\Z)$ for any
prime $p$.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
