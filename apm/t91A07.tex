Let $f:X\rightarrow X$ be a smooth map of a manifold to itself. Assume
that for every $x\in X$ such that $f(x)=x$, $df_x$ does not have $+1$
as an eigenvalue. Show that the fixed points of $f$ are isolated (\ie\
each fixed point is in some open set containing no other fixed
point). Hint: look at $f(x)-x$ in a coordinate chart. (This hint
doesn't  make sense, but that's ok.)
