Let $(\Omega,\Sigma,\mu)$ be a finite measure space and
$f_n:\Omega\rightarrow \R$ ($n=1,2,3,\ldots $) a sequence of
$\Sigma$-measurable functions. Prove that $\lim_{n\rightarrow
\infty}f_n(x)=0$ (in $\mu$-measure) if and only if
$$\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\int_{\Omega}|\arctan f_n(x)|d\mu(x)=0.$$
