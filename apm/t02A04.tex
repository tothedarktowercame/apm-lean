Let $\gamma$ be the simple closed curve on the boundary of the solid
torus $D^2\times \Son$ that starts on top, wraps around the hole two
full times, then passes around the back of the torus and connects back
up where it started. Let $X$ be the space obtained by attaching a
$2$-cell to $D^2\times \Son$ along $\gamma$. Prove that any map
$f:X\rightarrow X$ has a fixed point.
