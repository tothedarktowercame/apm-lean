Let $X$ be a smooth manifold and let $Y\subset \Rth$ be the
$z$-axis. Let $f:X\rightarrow \Rth$ be a smooth map (which may or may
not be transversal to $Y$). Find a $2$-parameter family of maps
$\{F_{\alpha,\beta}:X \rightarrow \Rth\}$ (the indices lie in some
open subset $S\subset \Rtw$) with the following properties.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{alist}
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item The map $f$ is in the family.
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\item For almost every $s\in S$, $f_s$ is transverse to $Y$. 
%------------------------------------------------------------------*
\end{alist}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\soln{Sard?}
