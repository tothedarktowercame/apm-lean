## Evidence notes

### The model `Surface true g` (ConstructionTargets/Surfaces.lean)
- The carrier is `Disk = closedBall (0:ℂ) 1`. `edgePoint N k t = exp(2πi(k+t)/N)`, t ∈ [0,1]. So edge k is the boundary arc from vertex v_k = e^{2πik/N} to v_{k+1}, and the round disk with its boundary cut into N arcs is homeomorphic to the N-gon. `v_N = e^{2πi} = 1 = v_0`, so edge N−1 closes up correctly at t = 1. The parametrisation is injective on each open arc, and distinct edges share only their endpoints.
- With N = 4g, the relation glues edge 4j at t to edge 4j+2 at 1−t, and edge 4j+1 at t to edge 4j+3 at 1−t. Reading the boundary, this is a_j b_j a_j⁻¹ b_j⁻¹, so the full word is ∏_j [a_j,b_j], the standard word for Σ_g. Glued edges never share an endpoint (for example, edge 4j ends at v_{4j+1} and edge 4j+2 starts at v_{4j+2}), so no edge is folded onto itself.
- Vertices: at t = 0 and t = 1 the gluings give v_{4j} ~ v_{4j+3}, v_{4j+1} ~ v_{4j+2}, v_{4j+1} ~ v_{4j+4} and v_{4j+2} ~ v_{4j+3}. So v_{4j} ~ v_{4j+1} ~ v_{4j+2} ~ v_{4j+3} ~ v_{4j+4}. Chaining over j gives one vertex class. Then χ = 1 − 2g + 1 = 2 − 2g, as it should be.
- Interior points are glued to nothing. The topology is the quotient (`coinduced`) of the compact disk by the closed equivalence relation EqvGen(SurfaceRel). This is the textbook polygon model, so `Surface true g` is the closed orientable genus-g surface for g = 5, all g ≥ 2, and g = 2. (The degenerate case g = 0, the bare disk flagged in t95J03, is not used here. t00A03 keeps `2 ≤ g`.)

### Rev 2: t02A03
- Geometry. L and R are unit circles in z = 0 centred at (−6/5,0,0) and (6/5,0,0). They reach x = −1/5 and x = 1/5, so their distance is 2/5. `W = {infDist(·, L∪R) ≤ 1/4} = T_L ∪ T_R`, where T_• is the closed 1/4-tube about the circle. Since 1/4 < 1 (the circle's reach), each T_• is a solid torus.
- Overlap. In the plane, the annuli 3/4 ≤ ρ ≤ 5/4 meet only where the outer disks overlap. Their centres are 12/5 < 5/2 apart, giving a convex lens |x| ≤ 1/20 near the origin. The inner disks (radius 3/4) stop at |x| = 9/20, well clear of the lens. Over the lens, T_L ∩ T_R = {|z| ≤ min(h_L, h_R)} with h_• > 0 inside the lens and 0 on its boundary. This is a 3-ball meeting each ∂T_• in a disk. So W is the boundary-connected sum of two unknotted solid tori: a genus-two handlebody, a regular neighbourhood of a planar graph, with standardly embedded boundary.
- C. C = L ⊂ int T_L ⊂ int W, and C is the core of the left handle, as K = {0}×S¹ is in D²×S¹. Its distance to R is 2/5 > 1/4, so C ∩ T_R = ∅. In particular C misses the overlap ball B.
- Conclusions. W∖C = (T_L∖C) ∪ T_R with intersection B. T_L∖C ≃ T² (punctured-disk bundle over S¹, as in part (a)), T_R ≃ S¹, and B is contractible. All pieces are compact semialgebraic, so the closed cover is excisive. Hence W∖C ≃ T² ∨ S¹, with π₁ ≅ ℤ² ∗ ℤ, H₀ = ℤ, H₁ = ℤ³, H₂ = ℤ and Hₙ = 0 for n ≥ 3. The remaining conjuncts (X ≃ T², π₁(T²), and the π₁/Hₙ of the wedge model Y) are unchanged and concern the models only.
- Versus the tangent model (centres (±1,0,0), graded faithful in reopen-grades-3). Both give a genus-two handlebody and W∖C ≃ T²∨S¹. In the tangent model C ran through the right tube. The new C misses the other handle entirely, which better matches "going around one of the holes". So the new model is at least as faithful, and arguably more literal.
