# Blueprint for t95J03

## Triage and repair

Verdict: **unfaithful (narrower), repaired**.

The source ranges over closed surfaces.  In the registered encoding,
`T95J03SurfaceSpace (.orientable 0)` is `Surface true 0`.  But
`SurfaceRel true 0` asks for an index `j < 0`, so it is empty; consequently
the quotient makes no boundary identifications and is the closed disk, not
the sphere.  Thus the classification domain omits `S²`.  It also contains the
spurious coordinate `.nonorientable 0`, which is not a closed connected
surface in the classification theorem.  This is the defect identified by
`statement-registry/triage-2.md`; I agree with that grade.

`Refute.lean` compiles the key calculation
`forall x y, not (SurfaceRel true 0 x y)`.  This is a faithfulness
counterexample rather than a counterexample to the displayed equality (the
old equality may still be true on its narrower, malformed domain).

`RevisedMain.lean` makes the smallest model-level repair recommended by the
grader:

* `.orientable 0` is realized by the unit sphere in `ℝ³`;
* a nonorientable genus is a `PNat`, eliminating genus zero; and
* every positive-genus coordinate keeps the existing polygon model.

The cover relation and conclusion are unchanged.  In particular, no
hypothesis encoding Euler-characteristic or orientability conclusions has
been introduced.

Degenerate checks: the repaired sphere case cannot enter the answer, since a
positive finite cover of it has positive Euler characteristic whereas every
finite cover of the Klein bottle has Euler characteristic zero.  The invalid
nonorientable-zero case is unrepresentable.  Degree zero is excluded inside
the library lemmas by `0 < k`, derived in the main theorem from `1 < k`.

## Remaining lemmas

### `t95J03_shared_cover_classification` — LIBRARY

**Statement in words.** For positive `k`, any classified closed surface which
shares a `k`-sheeted cover with the Klein bottle is either the torus or the
Klein bottle.

**Why true.** Decompose a possibly disconnected finite cover into path
components.  Euler characteristic is additive over the components and
multiplies by covering degree; homeomorphic total spaces have equal Euler
characteristic.  Since `chi(K)=0`, cancellation by positive `k` gives
`chi(F)=0`.  The closed-surface formula gives genus one in the orientable case
and genus two in the nonorientable case.  For the repaired sphere coordinate,
`chi(S²)=2`, so it is excluded rather than silently absent.

Mathlib currently has no end-to-end theorem combining finite-cover Euler
characteristic with the polygon models.  The closest sorry-free foundations
are `ConstructionTargets.Surfaces`, `CoveringDegree`, and
`RestrictPathComponent`; the polygon-surface homology program named in the
batch instructions is the natural source for Euler characteristic.  The
currently sorry-free `SurfaceClassification.lean` only constructs finite disk
covers and does not yet prove the classification invariant needed here.

**Expected size.** 500–1200 lines (library program, not a lemma-seat task).

**Dependencies.** Definitions only; intended to use the polygon-surface
homology foundation when delivered.

**Truth check.** Hand calculation: `k chi(F)=k chi(K)=0`; for `k>0`,
`chi(F)=0`.  The formulas `2-2g=0` and `2-g=0` give `g=1` and `g=2`.
At `g=0`, the repaired sphere has Euler characteristic two and is rejected.

### `t95J03_klein_self_share` — LIBRARY

**Statement in words.** For every positive degree, the Klein bottle shares a
cover with itself.

**Why true.** In the standard presentation
`pi_1(K)=<a,b | bab^-1=a^-1>`, the subgroup generated by `a` and `b^k` has
index `k`; the covering-subgroup correspondence supplies the connected
`k`-sheeted cover.  Use the same cover on the two sides and the identity
homeomorphism of its total space.

The closest sorry-free infrastructure is
`ConstructionTargets.DeckGroupQuotientFundamentalGroup` and
`SuppliedCoverAlgebra`.  What is missing is the explicit Klein-bottle action,
its index-`k` subgroup cover, and transport to the frozen polygon model.

**Expected size.** 350–700 lines (library program).

**Dependencies.** Definitions only.

**Truth check.** For `k=1`, the identity cover is the construction.  For
`k=2`, the subgroup `<a,b²>` is the torus orientation cover.  The index
calculation gives exactly `k` for every positive `k`.

### `t95J03_torus_share_iff_even` — LIBRARY

**Statement in words.** For positive `k`, the torus and Klein bottle share
homeomorphic `k`-sheeted covers exactly when `k` is even.

**Why true.** Every cover of the torus is orientable.  Each orientable
connected component of a Klein-bottle cover factors through the orientation
double cover and hence has even degree.  Therefore a homeomorphic total space
forces the sum `k` of component degrees to be even.  Conversely, for
`k=2m`, an index-`m` cover of the torus composed with its orientation double
cover gives a connected orientable `k`-cover of the Klein bottle; compare it
with a degree-`k` torus cover.

The same covering-subgroup foundations are imported.  The missing result is
the orientation-character criterion and the explicit cover constructions for
the frozen torus/Klein polygon models.

**Expected size.** 600–1400 lines (library program).

**Dependencies.** Definitions only.  It deliberately does not assume the
classification lemma, so all three library programs can run in parallel.

**Truth check.** Degree `2` is witnessed by the orientation double cover.
Degree `3` cannot work: a putative orientable Klein-cover would decompose into
components whose degrees are all even, but their sum would be three.  The
same parity argument covers disconnected total spaces.
