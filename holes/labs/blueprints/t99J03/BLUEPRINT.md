# Blueprint for t99J03

## Triage verdict and repair

**The registered Lean statement is false as written.**  The failure is the
orientable genus-zero model, not the classical classification theorem.
In the original `apm_t99J03_SurfaceRel true 0`, the witness would require a
natural number `j < 0`, so the generating relation is empty.  Its generated
equivalence relation is therefore equality and `apm_t99J03_Surface true 0` is
the closed disk with its original topology.

Take `X = Metric.sphere (0 : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)) 1`.  This is a compact,
connected Hausdorff smooth 2-manifold.  It is the orientable genus-zero closed
surface required by the problem, but it is not homeomorphic to the closed disk
(for example, the disk has boundary and the sphere does not).  Nor is it a
positive-genus orientable or nonorientable closed surface.  Thus neither
disjunct of the original theorem holds.  This is the counterexample recorded
by `holes/labs/statement-registry/triage-3.md`.  A short `Refute.lean` was not
included because formalizing the non-homeomorphism of sphere and disk is much
larger than the definitional diagnosis; the empty-relation calculation itself
does not alone refute the full disjunction.

The sentence of `problem.md` that the encoding got wrong is the classification
by connected sums of `g ≥ 0` tori: at `g = 0`, the empty connected sum is the
sphere, not the disk.

`RevisedMain.lean` makes the smallest local repair.  When `orientable = true`
and `genus = 0`, it uses the two-edge polygon word `a a⁻¹`: the two boundary
semicircles are identified with reversed parameters.  The disk quotient is the
sphere.  For positive orientable genus the old commutator word is unchanged,
and the nonorientable branch is unchanged.  Consequently the theorem retains
the same public type shape and now represents exactly the normal forms in the
problem statement.  The grader's sphere counterexample now lands in the
orientable branch with `g = 0`.

The repair's elementary sanity check is compiled as
`apm_t99J03_sphere_word_related`: every point on one boundary semicircle is
related to its reversed-parameter mate on the other.  Thus the repaired
genus-zero relation is not empty.  At `g = 1`, the definition still gives the
four-edge torus word, and at nonorientable `k = 1` it still gives the two-edge
projective-plane word.  The condition `0 < k` correctly excludes the empty
nonorientable sum.

## Remaining lemma

### `t99J03_compact_surface_classification` — LIBRARY

**Statement in words.** Every compact connected Hausdorff smooth 2-manifold is
homeomorphic either to exactly one repaired orientable polygon surface of genus
`g ≥ 0`, or to exactly one nonorientable polygon surface with `k > 0`.

**Why true.** This is the classical classification of closed connected
surfaces, specialized to the literal polygon quotient models in the repaired
statement.  Starting with a finite triangulation, cut along a dual spanning
tree to obtain one paired-edge polygon.  Edge slides and cancellations reduce
its word either to a product of `g` commutators or to `k` same-direction pairs.
The empty/cancellable orientable word gives the repaired sphere model.
Orientability separates the two families, and Euler characteristic (or
integral and mod-two homology) makes `g` or `k` unique.

The closest sorry-free foundation is
`ConstructionTargets/SurfaceClassification.lean`.  It currently reaches a
finite nested coordinate-triangle cover and formalizes finite two-complex and
boundary-arc infrastructure.  The topology worklist's current STOP is earlier
than classification: endpoint-relative parametric transversality must make
full coincidence fibers closed and discrete on the compact parameter square;
after that still come simultaneous perturbations, compatible face
intersections, a genuine triangulation, polygon cutting and word
normalization.  This is the shared surface-classification program named
`ConstructionTargets.SurfaceClassification`, so the missing theorem is stated
once as a LIBRARY boundary rather than divided into fake 30-line lemmas.

**Estimate.** 800+ lines / a multi-stage library program beyond the banked
construction; not a normal lemma seat.

**Depends on.** No other blueprint lemma.  The existing construction target is
its library foundation.

**Truth checks.** Hand classification gives: sphere at `g = 0` with `χ = 2`,
torus at `g = 1` with `χ = 0`, projective plane at `k = 1` with `χ = 1`, and
Klein bottle at `k = 2` with `χ = 0`.  The equal Euler characteristic of the
torus and Klein bottle shows why orientability is essential; the lemma keeps
the families separate.  The sphere counterexample to the old statement is now
accepted at `g = 0`.  Empty `X` is impossible under `ConnectedSpace X` in
Mathlib's convention used with a manifold structure, and the conclusion itself
supplies a nonempty model.

I am convinced the repaired LIBRARY statement is mathematically true.  The
doubt is implementation scale only: the available foundation is still well
short of a triangulation and polygon normal-form theorem.
