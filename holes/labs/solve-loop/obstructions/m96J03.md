# m96J03 proof obstruction

No counterexample was found; the statement is the standard unit-ball
Gauss--Green identity for the distributional Laplacian of an indicator.

The repository already proves measurability of the indicator, reduction of
the volume integral to the closed ball, the zero-dimensional case, and exact
identification of every frozen coordinate second derivative with the
corresponding diagonal value of the iterated Fréchet derivative.

For positive dimension the remaining assertion is precisely

`∫_closedBall Δφ = ∫_sphere Dφ(x)[x] dμH[n-1]`.

Mathlib's divergence theorem is for rectangular boxes.  Its polar integration
API uses `Measure.toSphere`, while the frozen statement uses restricted
Hausdorff measure; searches found neither a ball-specialized Gauss--Green
theorem nor an identification of these sphere measures in arbitrary finite
dimension.  Proving the bridge locally would require a new smooth-domain
divergence theorem or a polar-coordinate argument with differentiation under
the spherical integral and measure-normalization proofs.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
