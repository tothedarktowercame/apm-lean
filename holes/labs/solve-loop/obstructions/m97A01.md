# m97A01 proof obstruction

No counterexample was found; the theorem is the standard polar decomposition
and compact-adjoint result.

The repository already constructs the unique positive square root of `A†A`,
proves its norm identity, constructs the polar isometry on the algebraic range,
proves that isometry's generator formula and norm preservation, and defines
the canonical inclusion into the closure of the range.  Mathlib's
`LinearMap.extendOfNorm` can extend this map once the inclusion's dense range
is packaged; `denseRange_inclusion_iff` provides the main topological input.

The final conjunct remains independent: searches found no theorem that the
Hilbert-space adjoint of a compact continuous linear map is compact.  Proving
it locally requires developing either finite-rank approximation of compact
operators and compatibility with adjoints, or a total-boundedness argument
for the adjoint image.  Neither interface is packaged in this checkout.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
