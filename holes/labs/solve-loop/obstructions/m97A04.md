No counterexample was found; the finite-difference theorem is valid.

The repository already proves the homogeneous-boundary extension identities,
the exact diagonal/predecessor/successor coefficients, vanishing away from
those three positions, the two-neighbor norm identity under the mesh
smallness condition, strict diagonal norm greater than two, and unique
solvability from a nonzero determinant.

The remaining proof must explicitly collapse `Matrix.mulVec` over `Fin n` to
the three supported indices, with endpoint cases, and likewise collapse the
erased off-diagonal norm sum to at most the two neighbor entries.  A compact
bound on `a` then selects `h₀`, and the general Gershgorin theorem proves the
determinant nonzero.

Searches found `Matrix.det_ne_zero_of_sum_row_lt_diag` but no tridiagonal
`mulVec`, erased-row-sum, or determinant theorem that handles these dependent
`Fin n` neighbors.  The missing work is therefore a substantial local finite
sum assembly, not a direct library application.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
