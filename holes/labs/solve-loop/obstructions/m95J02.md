developments not present in the installed library:

   boundary limit proving `ΔΦ = δ`;

   fundamental-solution identity to the stated pointwise Poisson equation.

distributional Laplacians on Schwartz spaces, but searches found no radial
norm-composition formula, punctured-ball Green identity, or singular-kernel
