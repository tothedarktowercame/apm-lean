between the parameterized lower-triangular matrix and its diagonal matrix for

distinct diagonal entries is diagonalizable in the concrete matrix form
required here, and searches found no reusable Vandermonde/eigenvector
constructor.  Closing it locally requires defining four rational eigenvectors,
proving every denominator nonzero from the hypotheses, proving the resulting
matrix is a unit, and checking the symbolic conjugacy.

The other open conjuncts require specializing the normed-algebra exponential
series, truncating it using fourth-power nilpotence, and differentiating the
explicit polynomial flow coordinatewise.  These are finite but remain
separate developments; they do not resolve the missing conjugator.
