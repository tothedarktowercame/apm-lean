# m96A05 proof obstruction

No counterexample was found; the finite-difference theorem is valid.

The repository already proves all mesh-value coercion identities, the exact
three-neighbor support of each matrix row, uniqueness from a nonzero
determinant, and the strict lower bound `2 < ‖M i i‖` under `b < 0`.

Two finite but substantial assemblies remain:

1. expand `Matrix.mulVec` and collapse each dependent `Fin n` row sum to its
   predecessor, diagonal, and successor, including first/last-row Dirichlet
   substitutions, to identify the stencil with the matrix equation;
2. obtain a compact uniform bound for `a`, choose the mesh sufficiently fine,
   and prove the erased off-diagonal row sum is at most `2` by the same
   dependent predecessor/successor calculation.  Gershgorin's
   `Matrix.det_ne_zero_of_sum_row_lt_diag` then supplies nonsingularity.

Searches found only the general Gershgorin theorem, not a tridiagonal support
sum or determinant theorem matching this custom matrix.  Thus the missing
work cannot be discharged by a direct library call.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
