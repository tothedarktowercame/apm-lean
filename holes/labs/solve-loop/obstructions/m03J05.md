# m03J05 proof obstruction

No mathematical counterexample was found: the frozen statement is a valid
combination of standard one-dimensional Sobolev results.  It is nevertheless
not presently derivable from the APIs available in this `apm-lean` checkout
without developing substantial missing theory.

The first two conjuncts require a mixed Dirichlet--Neumann Fredholm theorem on
the custom space `ℝ × Lp ℝ 2 m03J05UnitMeasure`, including completeness of the
half-integer sine family and the exact one-dimensional resonant kernel.  The
repository proves only the pointwise eigenfunction equation and endpoint
conditions; Mathlib has no compatible Sturm--Liouville completeness theorem.

The third conjunct requires a bounded Sobolev reconstruction map for
`m03J05H1Value` and a Fréchet differentiability theorem for its integral
Nemytskii functional.  The fourth requires coercive monotone-operator
existence and uniqueness on the zero-trace subspace.  Searches of Mathlib and
`ConstructionTargets` found no declarations supplying these interfaces.

Consequently a complete `Main.lean` would have to formalize all three missing
theories in this single file.  No `sorry`, new axiom, imported placeholder, or
change to the pinned theorem has been used.
