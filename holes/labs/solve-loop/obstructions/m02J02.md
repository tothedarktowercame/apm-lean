No counterexample was found; the mathematical statement appears true.

The best partial proposal is `Main.lean`, copied from the pinned development. It
already proves the pointwise derivative, smoothness, and endpoint identities.
The remaining obligations are exactly the three bridge theorems:

1. Fourier-sine linear independence and graph-norm density in the custom
   `apm_m02j02_InH10 (Ioo 0 1)` encoding.
2. Linear independence, `H¹₀` membership via compactly supported smooth
   graph-norm approximation, and completeness of the odd Hermite-Gaussian
   family on `Ioi 0`.
3. The corresponding membership, linear independence, and graph-norm
   completeness of the full Hermite-Gaussian family on `Set.univ`.

Searches in Mathlib and this repository found Hermite polynomial identities,
Gaussian integrability, and differentiation lemmas, but no Fourier-sine or
Hermite completeness theorem that supplies these custom `eLpNorm` density
clauses. Proving them from first principles requires a substantial spectral
development rather than a local Lean repair.
