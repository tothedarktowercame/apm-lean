# m94J01 proof obstruction

No counterexample exists: the distributional assertion is standard and the
repository already proves the compact-support half.

The remaining tempered-distribution clause needs one Schwartz function whose
`n`th derivative at `n` is `1`.  The source constructs the correct disjoint
local summands and proves their diagonal jets, but packaging their infinite
locally finite sum requires both fields of `SchwartzMap`:

1. `ContDiff ℝ ∞` for the pointwise `finsum`;
2. for every fixed `k n`, a uniform bound on
   `‖x‖ ^ k * ‖iteratedFDeriv ℝ n f x‖`.

The installed Mathlib has `HasCompactSupport.toSchwartzMap` only for a single
compactly supported function.  Searches found no theorem turning a locally
finite family of smooth compactly supported functions into a `SchwartzMap`,
nor a Schwartz/Borel interpolation theorem for prescribed jets.  Therefore a
complete proof requires a new quantitative translated-polynomial/factorial
estimate and a locally finite differentiation development in this file.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
