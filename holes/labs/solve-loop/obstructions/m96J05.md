# m96J05 proof obstruction

No counterexample was found.  The statement is the standard Fourier-multiplier
proof of convergence of exponentiated symmetric differences to translation.

The repository already proves pointwise convergence of the symbols, their
modulus-one bounds, convergence after multiplication by arbitrary Fourier
data, and by induction that every finite operator power acts almost everywhere
as the corresponding scalar multiplier power.

The remaining bridge must pass from these finite identities through the
operator-norm `tsum` defining `NormedSpace.exp`, identify its application to an
`Lp` element almost everywhere with the scalar exponential series, and then
apply `L²` dominated convergence.  Point evaluation is not well-defined on
`Lp`, so `map_exp` cannot be used.  Searches found no bounded-measurable
multiplier algebra homomorphism into continuous operators on `L²`, nor a
theorem commuting a continuous-linear-map `tsum` with `Lp` representatives.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
