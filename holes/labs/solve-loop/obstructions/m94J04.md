# m94J04 proof obstruction

No mathematical counterexample was found.  The repaired field has the stated
distributional curl, and the repository already proves the coordinate inner
products, affine-slice derivative identity, and slice isometry.

The remaining scalar equality needs a measure-preserving transport from
`EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)` through its `WithLp` representation to a coordinate
product, followed by Fubini, compact-support transport to almost every affine
slice, and one-dimensional integration by parts.  Mathlib exposes the pieces
(`volume_preserving_measurableEquiv_toLp`,
`volume_preserving_piFinSuccAbove`, and `integral_prod`) but no theorem
combines them while rewriting the ambient `fderiv` evaluation and preserving
the test-function support hypotheses.

The usual global integration-by-parts lemma cannot be applied directly: the
coefficient `if 0 < p 0 then p 1 else p 0` is discontinuous across `p 0 = 0`,
even though the derivative directions used in the proof are tangent to that
interface.  Completing the proof therefore requires a new sliced Fubini
assembly rather than a local simplification.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
