# m95J07 proof obstruction

No counterexample was found.  The automatic-continuity conjunct is completely
proved in the repository by the sequential closed-graph theorem.  The source
also constructs the absolute-value cusp, proves its unit norm, and proves that
adding any nonzero scalar multiple of it to a differentiable function destroys
differentiability under the frozen ambient-extension predicate.

Those perturbation lemmas establish density of nondifferentiable functions,
but density does not imply nonmeagreness.  The missing Baire argument must
define countably many closed slope-control sets covering every function
differentiable at zero, prove each has empty interior using arbitrarily narrow
continuous spikes (a fixed small cusp has bounded slope and is insufficient),
and then apply the Baire theorem for the sup-norm `ContinuousMap` space.

Searches found no Mathlib or `ConstructionTargets` theorem asserting that
functions nondifferentiable at a fixed point form a nonmeagre subset, nor a
packaged closed slope-control construction compatible with the existential
ambient-extension definition used here.

No pinned declaration was altered and no placeholder or extra axiom was used.
